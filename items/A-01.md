## Statement

There are natural numbers `C, A` such that for every even `e ≥ 2` and every finite set of primes `ℓ ≥ 5` with
`(ℓ − 1) ∤ 2e`, we have `Σ_ℓ log ℓ · V_ℓ(1, e) ≤ C·(e + 1)^A`. Here `V_ℓ(1, e)` is the normalized length of a Selmer
group of `ℚ_ℓ/ℤ_ℓ(−e)` over `ℚ`. A comparison with K-theory, which is not in the Lean source, identifies it with
`v_ℓ(#K_{2e}(ℤ))`.

More precisely, `bound` says the following. For a prime `ℓ`, let `O` be the ring of integers of a finite extension of
`ℚ_ℓ`, with ramification index `e_O`, and let `ψ` be a Dirichlet character mod `N` with values in `O`. Let `Ш(ψ, e)`
be the set of classes in `H¹(Gal(ℚ̄/ℚ), (O ⊗ ℚ_ℓ/ℤ_ℓ)(ψ⁻¹χ_ℓ^{−e}))` that vanish on all inertia groups and on the
decomposition groups above the primes dividing `ℓN`, and put `V_ℓ(ψ, e) = length_O Ш(ψ, e) / e_O`. These lengths are
finite. The bound is for `ψ = 1` and `N = 2`, and for any choice of the rings `O`. If `ℓ ≥ 5`, `e ≥ 2` is even and
`(ℓ − 1) | 2e`, then the group defined in the same way but with no condition above `2` is zero (`propZ`). It contains
`Ш(1, e)`, which is therefore zero.

The character form (`corGK`) is the inequality
`Σ_{ℓ∈L} log ℓ · Σ_{ker ψ = H} V_ℓ(ψ, e) ≤ 2c·φ([(ℤ/N)^× : H])·(N(e + 1))^A·log(pp′)`, with `c, A` depending on `K`.
It holds for all `e`, `N` and `H ⊂ (ℤ/N)^×`, all primes `p ≠ p′` prime to `30·N·d_K`, and all finite sets `L` of
primes with some `ψ` of kernel `H` admissible (`GD`). Admissibility asks that `K` be imaginary quadratic,
`ℓ ∤ 2·d_K·h_K`, `N` even and prime to `d_K`, `ℓ ∤ ord ψ`, `ψ_ℓ² = 1` where `ψ_ℓ` is the `ℓ`-component of `ψ`,
`ψ(−1) = (−1)^e`, `(ℓ − 1) ∤ 2e`, and `K ⊄ ℚ(μ_ℓ)·ℚ(ψ/ψ_ℓ)`.

## Context

For odd `ℓ`, Poitou–Tate duality and the comparison of K-theory with étale cohomology (Weibel 2005, Theorem 70) relate
the length of `Ш(1, e)` over `ℤ_ℓ` to `v_ℓ(#K_{2e}(ℤ))`. To get equality one needs in addition that over `ℚ` the
classes that are trivial above `ℓ` make up the whole group, and we have not checked this step against a published
source. The identification is not part of the formal proof. Granting it, the two statements over `ℚ` (the bound and
`propZ`) bound `log #K_{4k}(ℤ)`, away from `2` and `3`, by a polynomial in `k`. These groups are tied to the even
eigenspaces of class groups of `ℚ(μ_ℓ)`, which Vandiver's conjecture predicts to vanish. Under the same identification
the character form concerns parts of wild kernels of abelian fields.

## Comparison with the literature

We compared the published results with a differently phrased statement, a bound for the order of `K_{2e}(ℤ)` away from
`2`, `3`, `7` and the primes with `(ℓ − 1) | 2e`. The Lean bounds Selmer classes, with `ℓ = 7` included, and to read
them as `v_ℓ(#K_{2e}(ℤ))` one needs Poitou–Tate duality for these groups and the comparison of K-theory with étale
cohomology. The source does not make this identification: it has Poitou–Tate statements for a differently defined
group, and no K-group occurs in the statement. So what follows bears on the Lean statement only through that
identification. Through it, the bound improves on the published bounds we found. These are bounds for K-groups and for
étale cohomology groups. For `log #K_{2e}(ℤ)` they are exponential in a power of `e` (Soulé 1999, Theorem 3; Soulé
2003, Proposition 4(iv); de la Torre Durán, a 2026 preprint), and they set aside the primes `5 ≤ ℓ ≤ 4e + 2`. The
improvement is asymptotic. Soulé's constants are explicit, while those of the preprint and those here are not, so
Soulé's explicit bounds are not recovered. Where they apply, `K_4(ℤ) = K_8(ℤ) = 0` and Vandiver's conjecture, which
has been verified below `2^31`, are stronger, and the bound does not imply Vandiver's conjecture. The vanishing case
is known in substance (Weibel 2005, Theorem 105). The local condition above `2` is automatic, and since the Lean
covers `ℓ = 7`, nothing requires excluding `7`.

An independent second check agrees that the bound improves on these, since it grows polynomially where they grow
exponentially, but it is less sure than we are that nothing closer is in print. We are less sure of the improvement
for the character form than for the bound over `ℚ`. We would like to know whether `C` and `A` can be computed from the
proof, and whether the Iwasawa-theoretic literature bounds the even-character parts for abelian fields.

## Sketch of the proof

*From characters to ℚ, and two primes.* `bound` is `bound_of` applied to `corGK`, the character form with `N = 2` (so
only the trivial character), `K = ℚ(√−3)`, `p = 7` and `p′ = 11`. Each `ℓ` in the sum is admissible because
`d_K = −3`, `h_K = 1`, and `3` ramifies in `K` but not in `ℚ(μ_ℓ)`. From `Bound`, `large_of` deduces that if `e_O = 1`
and `log ℓ > C(e + 1)^A` then `Ш(1, e) = 0`. This is an implication, and its conclusion is not declared as a separate
theorem. The character form follows (`U3.corGK`) from `PropGK`, which says that for each prime `p ∤ 30·N·d_K` there is
an integer `NN_p ≠ 0` with `log|NN_p| ≤ c·φ([(ℤ/N)^× : H])·(N(e + 1))^A·log p` and `Σ_ψ V_ℓ(ψ, e) ≤ 2·v_ℓ(NN_p)` for
every admissible `ℓ ≠ p`. Each `ℓ` differs from `p` or from `p′`, and `Σ_ℓ log ℓ · v_ℓ(n) ≤ log|n|`.

*The integer.* `propGK_of_numberU3U_of_oneCharU` takes a place `w` of `K` above `p`, `q = Nw`, a Frobenius `σ` at `w`,
and for each `ψ` the Eisenstein trace `t_ψ = Ψ(σ) + 1 + Ψ(σ)⁻¹`, where `Ψ = χ_ℓ^e ψ`, so that `Ψ(σ) = q^e ψ(q)`. Call
`ρ : G_K → GL₃(ℚ̄_ℓ)` *admitted* if either it is irreducible and `ρ ⊗ χ_ℓ⁻¹` is attached to a conjugate self-dual
cuspidal automorphic representation of `GL₃(𝔸_K)` of level `N`, with exponents `(e, 0, −e)` and unlinked at `ℓ`, or
`ρ ≅ W|_{G_K} ⊕ 1` for an irreducible two-dimensional `W` of `G_ℚ` of level `N·|d_K|` with `W ⊗ χ_ℓ^{−e}` attached to
a newform of weight `2e + 1`, together with a condition on the determinant of `W` and a clause on its restrictions to
imaginary quadratic fields. Two statements are combined. The first, `OneCharU_s`, says that if `(ψ, e, ℓ, K)` is
admissible and `tr ρ(σ) ≠ t_ψ` for all admitted `ρ`, then `V_ℓ(ψ, e) ≤ 2·Σ_{ρ∈X} v_ℓ(tr ρ(σ) − t_ψ)` for a finite set
`X` of pairwise non-isomorphic admitted `ρ`. The second, `NumberU3U_s`, gives an integer `NN ≠ 0` of the size above
with `|NN|_ℓ` at most `∏_ψ ∏_{ρ∈X_ψ} |tr ρ(σ) − t_ψ|_ℓ` for all such sets and all `ℓ ∤ 2p`. For this,
`numberU3U_of_prints` gives every admitted `ρ`, injectively on isomorphism classes, an index in a finite
`Aut(ℂ)`-stable family `(s_i)`, `i ∈ S`, with `tr ρ(σ) = s_i`, `|s_i| ≤ √q + 2`, `q^e·s_i` integral and
`#S ≤ c(N(e + 1))^A`. Then `NN = ∏_{ψ,i} q^e(s_i − t_ψ)` is an integer, and it is nonzero because
`|t_ψ| ≥ q^e − 1 − q^{−e} > √q + 2` for `q ≥ 7`. The bound `√q + 2` comes from the Jacquet–Shalika bound for the
unitary twist. The count of `S` over `K` reduces (`card_k_of_printed_results`) to the cohomology of congruence
subgroups, by theorems of Clozel and Borel and a bound at level `3M`. Over `ℚ` it passes through auxiliary imaginary
quadratic fields (`countQ_alt`).

*One character* (`oneCharU_of_katoTwoW_rig_admI_automorphy`). After a reduction to `O` unramified, which uses
`ℓ ∤ ord ψ`, the proof studies lifts of `Ψ̄ ⊕ 1 ⊕ Ψ̄⁻¹` that are conjugate self-dual over `K`, trivial on the inertia
elements away from `ℓ` that act trivially residually, subject at `ℓ` to an ideal of the local lifting ring, and
normalized at two marked elements. The universal ring `S.R` exists (`exists_ring`), and `exists_model` gives a
`ℤ`-graded `O`-algebra `A → S.R`, graded by conjugation with `diag(t, 1, t⁻¹)`, with the diagonal lift as a point `x`,
a formal chart at `x`, and homogeneous equations. In weight `−1` there is one more equation than there are
coordinates, and in weights `1` and `2` one fewer.

- (a) By `vlen_le`, the `b`-torsion of `Ш(ψ, e)` embeds in normalized cocycles on the lower triangular entries of
  weight `−1`. Each of these gives a point derivation at `x` modulo `b` that lifts to `O`, so
  `length Ш(ψ, e) ≤ cotLen₋₁`, the length of the weight `−1` cotangent lattice at `x` modulo the weight `−1` equations
  (`LemmaM'`).
- (b) `LemmaM` (`main_holds`) says that under conditions (M1)–(M5), `cotLen₋₁ ≤ 2·Σ_ξ v(ξ(a))`. The sum is over the
  torus orbits of *two-signed* points, those killing neither all negative nor all positive weights, and `a` is an
  element of weight `0` with `x(a) = 0` and `ξ(a) ≠ 0` at these points. `model_upper` takes `a = tr r(σ) − tr r₀(σ)`.
  The proof (`core_holds`) squeezes an Euler characteristic on a multigraded ring between a count at `x` and local
  terms.
- (c) By `twoPt_shape`, the two-signed points are the lifts that are neither upper nor lower triangular. Each is
  irreducible on `G_K` or of the form `W ⊕ μ`, and two with isomorphic restrictions to `G_K` lie in one torus orbit.

The conditions are arithmetic. (M1) combines the counts of the model with the vanishing of tangent vectors of weight
`≤ 0` at `x`. Finiteness of the weight-zero ring (M2) and automorphy of both kinds of two-signed points make up
`AutomorphyUnramifiedCoefficientsAlt`. This is residually reducible automorphy lifting for this problem, with no
Steinberg place, obtained through `ends0_alt`. (M3) uses that a lift which is both upper and lower triangular is the
diagonal one, and this is a use of `ℓ ∤ h_K`. (M4), the proportionality of derivations at two-signed points, follows
from the vanishing of adjoint Bloch–Kato Selmer groups (`RigidityFirstCase`, and for `W ⊕ μ`, `RigiditySecondCase`
together with Kato's theorem).

*The case where ℓ − 1 divides 2e* (`propZ`). It suffices to kill classes of order `ℓ`. If `(ℓ − 1) | e`, such a class
is a homomorphism unramified outside `ℓ`. It dies on the kernel of `χ_ℓ` by Kronecker–Weber, and restriction to that
kernel is injective. Otherwise `ℓ ≡ 1 mod 4`, the restriction of the class to `ℚ(√ℓ)` factors through the class group,
and `h(ℚ(√ℓ)) < ℓ` (`hQuad`, from `h ≤ √d·(1 + log √d)`).

## Key steps

- **Graded cotangent bound** (`UpperTheory.V.Alg.M.main_holds`): `MainCotSpan.lean`, line 32. With one more equation
  than coordinates in weight `−1`, the weight `−1` cotangent length at the fixed point is at most twice the sum of the
  valuations of a weight-zero element at the two-signed points. This is where Selmer lengths become trace differences.
- **Cotangent bound on the deformation model** (`UpperTheory.V.U3.model_upper`): `UnitaryThreeGroups/ModelUpper.lean`,
  line 103. It derives the five conditions for the graded model of the lifts of `Ψ̄ ⊕ 1 ⊕ Ψ̄⁻¹` and applies the bound
  to the trace at `σ`. With `vlen_le` (`VlenLe.lean`, line 52) the Selmer length is then bounded by trace differences.
- **Bound for one character** (`UpperTheory.V.Z.J.oneCharU_of_katoTwoW_rig_admI_automorphy`):
  `DenseThresholdCases/FOneCharU.lean`, line 75. It proves `V_ℓ(ψ, e) ≤ 2·Σ v_ℓ(tr ρ(σ) − t_ψ)` from nine named
  inputs: cohomology, three algebra lemmas, Kato's theorem, two rigidity statements, admissibility and automorphy.
- **Automorphy on the Eisenstein component** (`UpperTheory.V.Aut.AutomorphyUnramifiedCoefficientsAlt`):
  `DenseThresholdCases/AutomorphyUnramifiedCoefficients.lean`, line 23. This is a proposition, saying that the
  weight-zero ring is finite and the two-signed points are automorphic. It is derived at `CohInp.lean`, line 18, from
  two named propositions, which the proof of `corGK` supplies. No declaration states it without hypotheses.
- **The congruence integer** (`UpperTheory.GalN.V.Win.frobenius_trace_norm_nonzero_log_bound`):
  `AdmissibleResidues/FrobeniusTraceCountBoundOfRatEigensystemCount.lean`, line 329. From the family `(s_i)` it gives
  `NN ≠ 0`, `log|NN| ≤ #orbit·#S·(2e·log q + 1)` and the `ℓ`-adic inequality. Since `#S` is polynomial, so is
  `log|NN|`.
- **Specialisation to the rationals** (`UpperTheory.V.SmallClassNumbers.bound`): `SmallClassNumbers/CorGKOf.lean`,
  line 27, by `bound_of` (`ClassNumberThree.lean`, line 88). It takes `N = 2`, `K = ℚ(√−3)`, `p = 7`, `p′ = 11` and
  checks admissibility for every `ℓ ≥ 5` with `(ℓ − 1) ∤ 2e`. This choice fixes the primes covered and the condition
  above `2`.

## Caveats

`bound` and `corGK` are declared as theorems, without hypotheses, and they prove `Bound` (`ClassNumberThree.lean`,
line 16) and `U3.CorGK` (`KatoUse.lean`, line 44). `Main.lean` imports them, but its five final theorems do not use
them. We read in full the specialisation to `ℚ`, the two-prime and orbit steps, the integer `NN`, `model_upper`, the
proof for one character and most of `propZ`. We read only as statements the automorphy input and everything below
`ends0_alt`, the rigidity statements, Kato's theorem, the count of automorphic representations, the ring and the
model, and the inner lemmas of `LemmaM`. Published theorems enter as named propositions, gathered in the bundles
`HypothesesH'` and `LiteratureRankNHypothesis`. Each is supplied by a theorem elsewhere in the source, and we did not
read those. A text search found no `axiom` declaration in the source, and no `sorry` outside `Challenge.lean`, a file
of statements added for checking. We compiled nothing.
