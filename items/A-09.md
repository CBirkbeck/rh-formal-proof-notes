## Statement

`E`: a CM field, abelian over ℚ, unramified over `E⁺`, containing `K = ℚ(√−d)`, `d ≡ 7 mod 16` prime; `Φ`: the CM type
induced from `K`; `Sh`: a unitary Shimura variety of signature `(p, q)` at every `τ ∈ Φ`, `N = p + q`. `I_c(B, F)`: the
regularised arithmetic intersection number, per unit degree, of `F` (degree `D + 1`, `D = |Φ|N(N−1)/2`, in the lines
`x_{τ,i}` with Hodge metrics) on a model of a cover of the flag bundle of the component `c` with Hodge–Tate lattices at
the primes `≤ B` or dividing `2·disc(E)·level` (bad primes), Lie lattices elsewhere. The statement is for every tower of
such models tied to a model `Y` of `Sh` by the source's conditions `GoodOrigin` and `Extra`, for `Y` and a level
satisfying its condition `IsGlobal` (in particular the level is good at every prime), and for every intersection system
with the properties listed below; `B₁` and the constant in `O(log B/B)` depend on the tower, and the existence of a
tower is a separate statement. `deg_Fl`, `ĥ_Fl`: degree and arithmetic intersection number on the flag variety of
`∏_τ GL_N` over ℤ; degrees on `c` are `vol(c)·deg_Fl`. Assume `q ≥ 1`, `3q + 1 ≤ N`, at most `4|Φ|N²` bad primes. (a)
For `B ≥ B₁` there are reals `α_τ, β_τ` with `Σ_c I_c(B, m) = (Σ_c vol(c))·(ĥ_Fl(m) − κ(m)) + O(log B/B)` on monomials
`m`, where `κ = Σ_τ(α_τ − β_τ)·S^p_τ + Σ_τ β_τ·S^N_τ`, `S^p_τ(F) = deg_Fl(Σ_{i<p} ∂F/∂x_{τ,i})` and `S^N_τ` sums over
all `i`. (b) For real `u`, strictly decreasing in `i`, with `|u_{τ,a} − u_{τ,b}| ≥ g > 0` (`a ≥ p`, `b ≠ a`), and
integers `B ≥ 128`, `B + 1 > 4|Φ|N²(1 + ‖u‖_∞/g)`: the mean over `c` of `I_c(B, ℓ_u^{D+1})/((D+1)·vol(c)·deg_Fl(ℓ_u^D))`
is `≥ −C·(Λ·|u|₁ + q·spread(u))` with `C` absolute, `ℓ_u = −Σ u_{τ,i}x_{τ,i}`, `Λ = log(B·rd_E) + |(L′/L)(1, χ_d)|`
(`rd_E`: root discriminant of `E`; `χ_d`: quadratic character modulo `d`), `spread(u) = Σ_τ(max_i − min_i)u_{τ,i}`. (c)
If `|Φ| ≥ 2` and `u` is integral: at the cut-off `B = 4|Φ|N²(1 + ‖u‖_∞/g)`, the mean over the components of the volume
slopes of integral cusp forms of weight `u` is `≥ −C·((log B + log rd_E + |(L′/L)(1, χ_d)|)·|u|₁ + q·spread(u))`, the
volumes being upper limits along the weights `8k·u`; this is for a level good at every prime, given complex
uniformisation data for the components with the compatibilities the source asks (`CompLaws`, `UnifAll`), and when the
coefficient field is algebraically closed in the function field of each component. In the source (a) and (b) are the
conclusion of an implication from ten named statements, and (c) of an implication from six; see the last section.

## Why it might matter

The statement bears on an arithmetic form of Hirzebruch–Mumford proportionality: whether the top-degree arithmetic
numbers of automorphic bundles on a non-compact Shimura variety are those of the compact dual up to explicit
corrections. Here the correction has two real constants for each place and each cut-off, which the statement does not
determine (bounds for two averaged constants occur only inside the proof), and the numbers are limits on the models
above. It also bears on lower bounds, uniform in the field, for volumes of lattices of integral automorphic forms.

## What the checks found

The first reading compared a wider description: `p, q ≥ 1`, `N ≥ 3`, bounds for the two constants on the Shimura
variety, and a result it called unconditional. The statement here needs `3q + 1 ≤ N` and few bad primes for every part,
the proportionality included, does not export those bounds, and is an implication from named propositions, so it judged
a stronger statement. Its verdict is incomparable, medium confidence: published results are exact formulas in other
settings (Bruinier–Howard for `GU(n−1,1)`, Guo for compact `U(n,1)` in preprints, Maillot–Rössler 2018 for abelian
schemes), and published conjectures predict exact values in the non-compact case (Maillot–Rössler 2002 for semi-abelian
schemes with an action of the integers of a number field, which the first reading takes to include the case here; Köhler
for the Siegel case), where the description compared had two constants that were only bounded. With no Shimura variety
shared and the numbers not identified with the published ones, it compares shapes. A forward search (nine queries,
partial citation lists) found an arithmetic proportionality asked for by Hörmann (2011), its function-field analogue
proved by Feng, Yun and Zhang (2026, preprint), and nothing for `q > 1` or uniform in the field; verdict unchanged. The
second opinion, judging this statement: incomparable, medium confidence. Its corrections: keep `3q + 1 ≤ N`, the
averaging, tower-dependent thresholds and auxiliary hypotheses; Berman and Freixas i Montplet themselves distinguish
their lattices from an intrinsic formulation with integral cusp forms; and, where the two readings disagree,
"unconditional" does not follow from the statement, and reading it as a case of the Maillot–Rössler conjecture needs an
unproved comparison of intersection theories. Open: do the two constants take the predicted values, and are these
numbers the standard arithmetic intersection numbers?

## How the formal proof goes

*Abstract layer.* Models are horizontal multihomogeneous prime ideals over ℤ, with metrised lines; an intersection
system is any real function of such data that is symmetric and additive in the lines and obeys the recursion through the
divisor of a section and the integral of its log-norm, a projection formula, and vanishing on families pulled back from
lower dimension (`IsFullIntersectionSystem`). A `RootTower` is a family of models `M_B(c)` with lines `x_{τ,i}`, smooth
metrics on them depending on a parameter `t`, an ample line and a set of bad primes; `I_c(B, ·)` is the limit `t → 0`.
`chain_tower` derives (a) and (b) from properties of a tower: (i) `I8`: for a compact simple reflection `s`,
`I_c(B, ℓ_{sλ}^{D+1}) − vol(c)·ĥ_Fl(ℓ_{sλ}^{D+1})` is minus its value at `λ`. (ii) `ThmW`: on a compact part of each
non-compact simple wall, `|Σ_c I_c(B, ℓ_λ^{D+1})| ≤ C·log B/B`. In `thmW_tower` a rational weight on the wall is moved
off it by `θ = 5|Φ|/B`, `s = θ²` times an ample class is added and the metric is regularised at `t = exp(−1/s²)`. The
line is then semipositive and nef on every fibre (dominant weights are nef at the primes with Hodge–Tate lattices, and
at another prime `ℓ` when `4|Φ|·‖μ‖_∞ ≤ (ℓ − 2)·gap`), so both halves of Zhang's inequality of successive minima apply,
with heights of points `≥ −C·log(B/s)` everywhere (`I5`) and `≤ C·log B` on a dense set (`I6`), against a geometric
degree `O(θ)`; regularising costs `O(s)` (`I2`), returning to the wall `O(θ·log B)` (`I1`). (iii) `ThmB`: on `ℓ_x^{D+1}`
the difference `Σ_c I_c − vol_tot·ĥ_Fl`, `vol_tot = Σ_c vol(c)`, is a polynomial of degree `D + 1`, antisymmetric under
the compact simple transpositions by (i) and small on the walls by (ii), where `ĥ_Fl` vanishes; a quantitative lemma on
such polynomials puts it within `C·ε`, coefficient by coefficient, of the Vandermonde product times a linear form
invariant under the compact Weyl group, which is rewritten through `S^p_τ`, `S^N_τ`. (iv) `PropA`, a transitive group of
permutations of `Φ` preserving `Σ_c I_c`, lets `α_τ, β_τ` be replaced by their means. (v) `Prop6`: for `B₀ ≤ B`,
`I_c(B₀, p_k(τ)·Q) − I_c(B, p_k(τ)·Q)`, with `p_k(τ) = Σ_i x_{τ,i}^k`, is `vol(c)` times an explicit sum over the good
primes in `(B₀, B]`. Letting `B → ∞`, two test classes form a triangular system, so the corrected coefficients converge,
and `Σ_c I_c(B₀, p_k(τ)·Q) = vol_tot·(ĥ_Fl − A₀·Σ_σ S^p_σ − b₀·Σ_σ S^N_σ + rest)` holds exactly at every cut-off
`B₀ ≥ 2` (`exists_identAt`). (vi) With `Λ` as in (b) at `B₀` (`rd_E`: root discriminant; `χ_d`: quadratic character
modulo `d`): the class `Σ_i x_{τ,i}` is a flat line of height `O(N·Λ)` (`PropF`), so `|p·A₀ + N·b₀| ≤ C′·N·Λ` for
`B₀ ≥ 128` with at most `B₀` bad primes, `C′` the constant of `PropF` (4 for the Shimura towers); a lower bound for the
number of a power of the smaller Hodge determinant times the fibre class (`SlopeLower`) and an upper bound for the
height of a product of Grassmannians give `A₀ ≥ −C·Λ`. (vii) In `tower_T2_of_inputs`, `ℓ_u^{D+1}` is decomposed along
the `p_k(τ)`, the exact identity is applied term by term, `ĥ_Fl ≥ 0` on dominant weights, the remainder is at most
`2(D+1)·deg_Fl(ℓ_u^D)·q·spread(u)` by the condition on `B`, and `A₀·s + b₀·t = A₀·(s − pt/N) + (p·A₀ + N·b₀)·t/N`; equal
volumes then give the mean.

*Shimura layer.* `Sh` is the moduli scheme of abelian schemes with `𝒪_E`-action for a self-dual Hermitian `𝒪_E`-lattice
and an odd principal level `M ≥ 3` split in `ℚ(√−d)`, good at every prime; `Y` is a normal projective flat model of it.
`volumeTheoremX_of_inputs` proves the properties for every tower tied to `Y` by a `GoodOrigin`: its fields give a normal
scheme over `Y` with rational `ℓ⁴`-torsion at the primes `ℓ ≤ B` and the bad primes, on which the patched lattices are
vector bundles, a full flag of them, and the Hodge norms of its graded lines (`x_{τ,i}`, `0 ≤ i < N`: for `i < p` graded
pieces for `Lie_τ`, for `i ≥ p` duals of those for `Lie_{cτ}`). (i) comes from a relation on the fibre square of each
projective-line fibration that forgets one step of the flag, used on the model and on the flag variety over ℤ, and from
proportionality of geometric degrees. `E` abelian over ℚ is an argument of almost all the statements about the Shimura
towers that were read; whether their proofs use it was not checked. In (iv) the symmetry comes from automorphisms of the
moduli scheme attached to those of `E` (`TwistAut`). `3q + 1 ≤ N` is used for `SlopeLower` (also, as `q ≤ p`, in the
heights on the base that enter it) and for the equality of the `vol(c)`, which is part of the conclusion; the bound on
the number of bad primes, for heights on the base. That such a tower exists is a separate statement
(`GoodTowerExistsX`). For (c), `top_meanAll` takes `B = 4|Φ|N²(1 + ‖u‖_∞/g)` and bounds the volume slope on each
component (growth of the number of integral cusp forms of weight `k·u` of bounded norm) below by the slope of
`I_c(B, ℓ_u^{D+1})` minus `O(log B + log rd_E)·|u|₁`: small sections are counted by a volume inequality for semipositive
lines nef on the fibres and carried to integral forms.

*Inputs.* All these theorems are implications from named statements. General ones: an intersection system, Zhang's two
inequalities, arithmetic Hilbert–Samuel and volume inequalities, Riemann–Roch for nef classes, resolution of singularities, a
Hodge–Tate comparison for `p`-divisible groups (`HT.FarguesHodgeTate`), Zarhin's trick. `good_tower_volume_theorem_bis`
reduces (a), (b) to ten; for each the source has a declaration without hypotheses, or a derivation from such, not read.
