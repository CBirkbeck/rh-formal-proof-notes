## Statement

Let `χ` be a primitive Dirichlet character (conductor `f`, order `o`), `n ≥ 2`, and `q ≡ 3 mod 8`, `q′ ≡ 7 mod 16`
primes not dividing `f`. Put `N = lcm(2^{n+2}, f)·q·q′` and `E = ℚ(μ_{2^{n+2}})⁺·ℚ(χ)·ℚ(√−q)·ℚ(√−q′)`, where `ℚ(χ)` is
the fixed field of the kernel of `χ`; `E` is a CM field of conductor `N`. For a prime `ℓ` and `e ≥ 1`, `Ш¹(E, ℓ, e)`
is the group of classes in `H¹(G_S, ℚ_ℓ/ℤ_ℓ(−e))` vanishing on the decomposition groups above `ℓ`, where `G_S` is the
Galois group of the maximal extension of `E` unramified at the finite places not above `ℓ`; lengths are over `ℤ_ℓ`.
For odd `ℓ`, `Ш⁺`, `Ш⁻` are the `(−1)^e`- and `(−1)^{e+1}`-eigenspaces of complex conjugation. `B(χ)` is `{3}` with
the odd primes dividing `o·f·p·h·p₁`: `p` the least prime `≥ 7`, `≡ 3 mod 4`, `p ∤ f`; `h` the class number of
`ℚ(√−p)`; `p₁` the least prime `≡ 1` modulo the odd part of `o` with `p₁ ∤ 2fp`. Main case, the plus part (`SumOut`):
there are `C` and primes `r₁, r₂`, depending on `χ` only, such that for all `n, t ≥ 2` and all `q, q′` greater than
each of `2t+1, f, o, p, h, p₁, r₁, r₂`, the groups below have finite length and
`Σ_{ℓ odd, ℓ ∉ B(χ)} log ℓ · Σ_{1≤e<t} length Ш⁺(E, ℓ, e) ≤ (N·t)^C`. Other cases: the minus part at all odd `ℓ`, with
an explicit bound (`ThmBcFree`); the plus part at the primes of `B(χ)`, given bounds on fixed cyclotomic towers
(`PointHV`); at all primes, a bound on windows of scales only (`JointWin`, `VWin`).

## Why it might matter

By Poitou–Tate duality `Ш¹(E, ℓ, e)` is dual to the kernel of localisation on `H²(𝒪_E[1/ℓ], ℤ_ℓ(e+1))`, which for odd `ℓ`
is the `ℓ`-part of the étale wild kernel of `K_{2e}(𝒪_E)`; the formal statements concern `Ш¹` only and name no K-group.
The question is how large these groups can be, in terms of the conductor and the twist, as the field varies in a
family of growing degree. The source bounds the minus part through Bernoulli numbers, the plus part by other means.

## What the checks found

The first reading compared a stronger, differently phrased statement: a bound for all of `Ш¹` outside `B(χ)`, read as
a bound for the étale wild kernels of `K_{2e}(𝒪_E)`, without the condition `q, q′ > r₁, r₂`, with wider window bounds
and a tower form. The Lean has no K-groups, the two parts as separate propositions (the plus part with that
condition), no total, a narrower window bound and no tower form; the verdict bears on this page through the two parts.
Its verdict: improves, for this family of fields. At odd primes outside a finite set the bound is polynomial in
conductor and twist, one exponential below Bayer-Fluckiger–Emery–Houriet 2015 (Theorem 1.1) in degree and twist, and
further below Soulé 2003 (Theorem 3), small primes included; those bounds, for K-groups or étale cohomology, hold for
every number field with explicit constants, at large primes only. In the discriminant alone the shape is theirs; the
minus part is known in substance. Medium confidence. The second opinion: improves within this family, medium
confidence. It asks for the same changes as the comparison with the Lean (restore `r₁, r₂`; withdraw the claim for all
odd `ℓ` when `χ = 1`, the tower form and the wider windows), and two more: the two published ways of setting small
primes aside are not equivalent, where the first reading took them to agree, and summing over the twists is not new in
itself. The verdicts agree; the readings differ on these two points. Open: is a bound of this size for the plus part
in print for any family of growing degree? Is the minus-part bound stated character by character for CM fields?

## Nearest published work

- C. Soulé, *A bound for the torsion in the K-theory of algebraic integers*, Documenta Math., Extra Volume Kato
  (2003), 761–788, <https://ems.press/content/book-chapter-files/27335>. For every number field `F` of degree `r`:
  Theorem 2 bounds the torsion of `K_m(O_F)` prime to `p ≤ max(r + 1, 2m + 2)` by `k(m)`; Theorem 3 bounds the product
  of `#H²(O_F[1/p], ℤ_p(n))` over `p ≥ 4n − 1`, `p ≥ r + 2`, by `k(2n − 2)`; Proposition 4(i) bounds `log log k(m)`
  explicitly, with a factor `r^{4r}√D`. On the fields here this is triple exponential in the degree and leaves out the
  small primes; it holds for every field, explicitly, so neither statement implies the other. Read by the first
  reading; checked by the second opinion.
- E. Bayer-Fluckiger, V. Emery, J. Houriet, *Hermitian lattices and bounds in K-theory of algebraic integers*,
  Documenta Math., Extra Volume Merkurjev (2015), 71–83, arXiv:1406.5953, <https://arxiv.org/abs/1406.5953>. Theorem
  1.1: for `F ≠ ℚ` of degree `d`, the logarithm of the order of the torsion of `K_n(O_F)` modulo the subgroup
  generated by the elements of order at most `max(d + 1, 2n + 2)` is at most
  `(2n+1)^{71n⁴d³}·d^{293n⁵d⁵}·|D_F|^{528n⁵d⁴}`. On the fields here, at twist `e`: `exp(O(e⁵d⁵ log(dN)))` against
  `(Nt)^C`, at the large primes both cover; the same shape in the discriminant alone. Read by the first reading;
  checked by the second opinion, which adds that its way of setting small primes aside (a quotient by elements of
  bounded order) is not equivalent to Soulé's.
- V. Emery, *Torsion homology of arithmetic lattices and K₂ of imaginary fields*, Math. Z. 277 (2014), 1155–1164,

## How the formal proof goes

Form of the source. Each bound is a named proposition; theorems prove implications between them, with hypotheses supplied
along a chain ending at `vwin_of_row`. `sumOut` proves `SumOut` from case bounds `CountI`, `CountZ`, `CountII`, `CountIII`.

From the field to characters. `E` is the fixed field of an explicit subgroup `H` of `(ℤ/N)^×` (`hostLevel`); for odd
`ℓ ∉ B(χ)`, `ℓ ≥ 5` and `ℓ ∤ [E:ℚ]` (`bchiFacts`). Over a discrete valuation ring `O`, finite over `ℤ_ℓ`, with enough roots
of unity, `redPrime` (through `partModel`) gives `length Ш⁺(E, ℓ, e) ≤ Σ_ψ V_ℓ(ψ, e)`, over the characters `ψ` modulo `N`
trivial on `H` with `ψ(−1) = (−1)^e`; `V_ℓ(ψ, e)` is the `O`-length, divided by the ramification index, of the classes in
`H¹(G_ℚ, (Frac O/O)(ψ⁻¹χ_ℓ^{−e}))` unramified everywhere and zero on the decomposition groups at the primes dividing `ℓN`.

Case split (`cases`). For `ℓ ∉ B(χ)`: Case I if `(ℓ−1) ∤ 2e`; otherwise `e ≥ 2`, `ℓ ≤ 2t`, `ℓ ∤ N`, and the case is Z
(`ψ = 1`), II (order of `ψ` even) or III (order odd, `ψ ≠ 1`). The size conditions on `q, q′` are used here.

Case I (`CountI`). Fix `K = ℚ(√−p)`. For one `ψ` the source takes a deformation problem for three-dimensional conjugate
self-dual representations of `G_K` with residual constituents `Ψ̄, 1, Ψ̄⁻¹`, `Ψ = χ_ℓ^e·ψ`. Its universal ring, graded by a
torus, enters two statements of commutative algebra: `LemmaM′` (the Selmer group is no longer than the cotangent module
of weight `−1` at the base point) and `LemmaM` (that module has length at most twice the sum, over the orbits of
"two-signed" points `ρ`, of the valuation of `tr ρ(σ) − (Ψ(σ) + 1 + Ψ(σ)⁻¹)`, for a chosen `σ ∈ G_K`). These points are
shown to come from conjugate self-dual automorphic representations of `GL₃` over `K` of level `N`, or from newforms of
weight `2e+1` and level `N·|d_K|` (`OneCharU_s`), using rigidity and automorphy statements and a vanishing statement for
Selmer groups of twisted modular forms (`KatoTwoW`), proved elsewhere in the source. With `σ` a Frobenius above a prime
`r`, a count of these representations, polynomial in `N(e+1)`, and bounds on Hecke eigenvalues give an integer `NN ≠ 0` with
`log|NN| ≤ c·#𝒪·(N(e+1))^A·log r` and `Σ_{ψ∈𝒪} V_ℓ(ψ, e) ≤ 2·v_ℓ(NN)` for each Galois orbit `𝒪` of characters and each
admissible `ℓ ≠ r` (`PropGK`). Two primes `r₁ ≠ r₂` cover all `ℓ`, and `Σ_ℓ log ℓ·v_ℓ(NN) ≤ log|NN|` (`corGK`); they are
fixed for `χ` and must not divide `N`, hence the condition `q, q′ > r₁, r₂`.

Cases Z, II, III. `propZ`, a theorem without hypotheses: `Ш¹(ℚ, ℓ, e) = 0` for `ℓ ≥ 5`, `e` even, `(ℓ−1) | 2e`, using
`h(ℚ(√ℓ)) < ℓ` when `(ℓ−1) ∤ e`. `sp4Main_alt`: in Cases II and III, `V_ℓ(ψ, e)·log ℓ ≤ (ℓ·N·e)^C`, by the same mechanism
for a symplectic problem of rank four with an auxiliary character of 2-power conductor. Assembly (`sumOut`): for each
`e < t` the Case I primes are summed at once; the others are at most `2t` primes with at most `N` characters each.

Minus part (`thmBcFree_of`). For odd `ℓ ∤ [E:ℚ]`, `PsiBound` bounds the `ψ`-component of `Ш⁻` by the valuation of a
generalized Bernoulli number `B_{e+1,ψ}` times an explicit denominator; it is derived from Poitou–Tate duality and a
local orthogonality statement (`UpperTheory.V.ML.iv`). For the odd `ℓ | [E:ℚ]`, `StickBound`: a power of `ℓ` of bounded
exponent kills `Ш⁻`, whose `ℓ`-torsion has bounded length. Result, for any abelian `M` in place of `ℚ(χ)`, `d = [E:ℚ]`,
`C` absolute: `Σ_{ℓ odd} log ℓ · Σ_{1≤e<t} length Ш⁻(E, ℓ, e) ≤ 3d·t²·log(tN) + C·Σ_ℓ ℓ·d²·t²·log²(tℓN)`, the last sum over
the odd primes dividing `[M:ℚ]`. For odd `ℓ`, `length Ш¹ = length Ш⁺ + length Ш⁻` (`shaLen_eq_add`), so the parts add.

Bad primes, the prime 2, windows. For `ℓ ∈ B(χ)` the reduction to characters has a loss polynomial in `N·e` (`redAll`);
the components are in Case II, in Case V (`e = 1`: a unitary problem relative to `ℚ(i)`, or the bound for `H²` below),
or in Case IV, where `ψ` is bounded by `Ш¹` of a layer of the cyclotomic `ℓ`-tower of `ℚ(μ_{ℓf})`: hence `PointHV`,
with a hypothesis `B` on those layers. For such a tower over an abelian field containing `μ_ℓ` (`μ_4` if `ℓ = 2`),
`ABound` gives: among the twists `e ≤ Z` at most `R` are exceptional, and for the others `Ш¹` of the `m`-th layer has
length `O(m + log Z)`; `R` comes from the Ferrero–Washington theorem, proved in the source (`FW.ferreroWashington`).
At exceptional twists `Ш¹` is bounded by `H²(G_S, ℤ_ℓ(e+1))` for a fixed layer `L` (continuous cochains, `G_S` as in
the Statement, with `L` in place of `E`; its identification with étale cohomology of `𝒪_L[1/ℓ]` is not in the source),
where `LemmaK` gives `log log # ≤ (2e[L:ℚ])^C·log(2|d_L|)` for totally complex `L`. A pigeonhole on scales (`window`)
gives `Y ∈ [X^{1/J}, X^{1/2}]` such that for `x ∈ [Y, Y²]` no exceptional twist lies between `x^η` and `x^κ` (`κ ≥ 1`
the exponent bounding the twists, `t ≤ x^κ`, and `0 < η ≤ κ` a parameter); the exceptional terms are then at most
`exp(x^{4ε/5})` (`thmBab_of`). `JointWin` (`0 < ε < 1/52`, `κ = 401`): for large `X` and `q, q′` above `2X^κ+1` and a
constant, `log(qq′) ≤ X^{ε/(4J)}`, on such a window the 2-part is at most `exp(x^{4ε/5})` and the plus part at odd
primes at most `(N·t)^C + C·exp(x^{4ε/5})`, for `2^n ≤ x^α` (`α` any fixed real) and `t ≤ x^κ`. `vwinAt_of_joint` adds
the minus part: `VWin` bounds the total over all `ℓ` and `e < ⌊x^{401}⌋` by `√exp(x^ε)`, for
`x^{2+8ε} ≤ 2^n ≤ 2x^{2+8ε}` and pairs `q, q′` restricted in size and by congruences. The source has no single
declaration for all of `Ш¹` outside `B(χ)`, and none for fields without the primes `q, q′`.

## What was checked, and what was not

`SumOut`, `PointHV`, `ThmBcFree`, `JointWin`, `VWin` are definitions of propositions, and no theorem was found that states
one of them without hypotheses: `sumOut`, `point`, `thmBcFree_of`, `jointWin_of`, `vwinAt_of_joint`, `vwin_of_row` are
theorems with proofs whose hypotheses are other named propositions. The chain from `vwin_of_row` down to `sumOut` was
followed by reading: each hypothesis is supplied by a theorem of the source, that of `vwin_of_row` itself in
`src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/MainConclusion.lean`. Proofs read: `sumOut`, `point`, `thmHV`,
`jointWin_of`, `vwinAt_of_joint`, `shaLen_eq_add`, `vvle_of_parts`, `redPrime`, `cases`, `countZ`, `corGK`, `window`; the rest
(deformation rings, automorphy, counts, `LemmaM`, `PsiBound`, `StickBound`, `LemmaK`, `ABound`) as statements only. A text
search of all Lean files found `sorry` only in `src/Challenge.lean` (imported by no file) and no `axiom`; nothing was compiled.
