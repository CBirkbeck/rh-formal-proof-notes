## Statement

Let `E` be a CM field, `a ≥ 1`, `π` a regular algebraic cuspidal automorphic representation of `GL_a(𝔸_E)` and `χ` a
Hecke character of `E⁺` with `(π, χ)` polarized. For every prime `ℓ` and `ι : ℚ̄_ℓ ≅ ℂ` the formal proof constructs a
continuous semisimple `r : Gal(Ē/E) → GL_a(ℚ̄_ℓ)`. At `w ∤ ℓ` its Weil–Deligne representation has the rank of the
parameter attached to `π_w ⊗ |det|^{(1−a)/2}` by any map with the laws the source requires of a local correspondence
(the first reading did not unfold these laws), and equals it when that parameter is abelian with `N = 0` (so at
unramified places Frobenius has the Satake polynomial); a tame case with `E_w ≅ ℚ_p` and `p > 16a²` (or `a = 1`) is also
treated, under a multiplicity condition on inertia and with a weaker conclusion (`N = 0` and the characteristic
polynomials on inertia). At `w | ℓ` with `π_w` spherical it is crystalline with `τ`-Hodge–Tate weights `(a−1)/2 − z`,
`z` in the infinitesimal character of `π` at `ι∘τ`; in the abelian and tame cases above with `N = 0` it is de Rham and
potentially semistable with these weights. At the other places above `ℓ` no Hodge–Tate weights are asserted. This is a
weaker form of the published theorem (Shin, Chenevier–Harris and others): polarization of `r`, purity and local–global
compatibility at general ramified places are absent, and the published statement is written in the source as a
proposition for which the first reading found no proof. A companion theorem treats the definite unitary group of the
standard Hermitian form, for `E/E⁺` unramified at every finite place and `Π` unramified above `ℓ`, as in Guerberoff's
Theorem 2.3. It is proved as an implication from eight named propositions (six published statements, a form of the
`GL_a` statement above, and the existence of a map with the laws of a local correspondence), which the first reading
reports are discharged elsewhere in the source.

## Why it might matter

The published constructions that the first reading could consult compare point counts on Shimura varieties with a
stabilised trace formula. The formal proof does not; it uses instead a holomorphic fixed-point formula and a trace
comparison on the definite unitary group, a congruence relation and Sen's operator. The comparison theorems of `p`-adic
Hodge theory that it needs are proved in the source, not cited.

## What the checks found

The first reading finds the argument different from the published constructions it could read at two steps: placing
the eigensystem of a form on the definite group in Hodge cohomology by the holomorphic Lefschetz formula, and above all
cutting a rank-`a` representation out of étale cohomology with lacunary Hodge–Tate weights, Sen's operator and a
determinant character. Even rank by two theta lifts is a different means to a known end; the other steps are standard in
kind. Verdict for a weaker form of the theorem, medium confidence: deep proofs not read, literature through abstracts and
introductions, four searches; finding no antecedent within those limits does not establish novelty. The second opinion
cannot tell (medium confidence): the ingredients are established, but getting an actual rank-`a` representation from
annihilating polynomials is much stronger than bounding eigenvalues and cannot be dismissed as routine. It corrects the
first reading: Chenevier–Harris treat even rank by interpolation; Tu separates correspondences from Atiyah–Bott's theorem
for maps; ordinary density alone does not give an identity of correspondences; and the proof does enumerate fixed points
and compare traces. The opinions disagree. Open: is the rank-extraction lemma, or an equivalent, in print? Has a
Lefschetz trace of Hecke correspondences been used before to place definite-group eigensystems in cohomology?

  cohomological cuspidal `Π` over CM fields, with local–global compatibility away from `ℓ` and Ramanujan–Petersson, by
  Igusa varieties, a stabilised point-counting formula and stable and twisted trace formulas. Abstract only.
- G. Chenevier, M. Harris, *Construction of automorphic Galois representations, II*, Cambridge J. Math. 1 (2013), 53–73,
  <https://www.intlpress.com/site/pub/files/_fulltext/journals/cjm/2013/0001/0001/CJM-2013-0001-0001-a002.pdf>. The cases
  outside Shin's hypotheses, even rank included, by interpolation on definite unitary groups with partially varying
  weights, and patching. Not opened by the first reading (known through Caraiani's introduction); checked by the second
  opinion.
- A. Caraiani, *Local-global compatibility and the action of monodromy on nearby cycles*, Duke Math. J. 161 (2012),
  arXiv:1010.2188, <https://arxiv.org/abs/1010.2188>. Records who constructed the representations in which
  cases; its own method (Igusa varieties after Shin, the weight spectral sequence) has no congruence relation and no
  holomorphic Lefschetz formula. Introduction read, in summary form.
- L. Guerberoff, *Modularity lifting theorems for Galois representations of unitary type*, Compositio Math. 147 (2011),
  1022–1058, arXiv:0906.4189, <https://arxiv.org/abs/0906.4189>. §2, Theorems 2.2 and 2.3: for a totally
  definite unitary group, Labesse's base change and the Mœglin–Waldspurger description give the representation as a sum
  over Speh segments, the `GL_m` representations being a black box. The counterpart of the definite-group theorem here.
  §2 read, in summary form.
- O. Bültel, T. Wedhorn, *Congruence relations for Shimura varieties associated to some unitary groups*, J. Inst. Math.
  Jussieu 5 (2006), 229–261, arXiv:math/0202026, <https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0202026>. The congruence relation for
  `GU(n−1,1)`, `p` inert, `n` even, by density of the `μ`-ordinary locus in the space of `p`-isogenies, after
  Faltings–Chai and Wedhorn (whose split PEL case of 2000, the nearest counterpart of the relation used here, was not
  opened). Introduction read by the first reading; §8 checked by the second opinion.
- B. Moonen, *Serre–Tate theory for moduli spaces of PEL type*, Ann. Sci. ENS 37 (2004), 223–269,
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0203288>. §4.2: congruence relations on the ordinary locus, extended when the ordinary
  isogeny locus is dense. Checked by the second opinion; known to the first reading through Koskivirta's introduction
  (arXiv:1211.1758).
- S. Y. Lee, *Semisimplicity of étale cohomology of certain Shimura varieties*, arXiv:2206.07283,
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.07283>, reproducing the criterion of K. Fayad, J. Nekovář, *Semisimplicity of certain
  Galois representations occurring in étale cohomology of unitary Shimura varieties* (Amer. J. Math. 141 (2019),
  503–530): congruence relations and that criterion (strong irreducibility, a Cartan subalgebra acting without
  multiplicities, from Sen's theorem and distinct Hodge–Tate weights, and annihilation on a dense set) give

- L. W. Tu, *Lefschetz fixed point theorems for correspondences*, arXiv:2207.00384, <https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.00384>.
  The holomorphic Lefschetz formula and its proof by Atiyah–Bott; computing the trace of a Hecke correspondence this way
  is posed as an open problem. Abstract only (first reading); the second opinion cites §§7–8,
  which separate the problem for correspondences from Atiyah–Bott's theorem for maps.

## How the formal proof goes

Seed case (`GeoOne`). `a` is odd; `E` contains an imaginary quadratic field `ℚ(√−d)` in which `ℓ` splits, `[E⁺:ℚ]` is
even, and the non-split places are unramified and prime to `ℓ`. `U` is an automorphic representation of the definite
unitary group in `a` variables that contains, at a split place `w₀ | ℓ`, a character of `ℓ`-power order of a compact
open subgroup forcing supercuspidality, and whose weight at another place `v′ | ℓ` is lacunary: no two disjoint non-empty
sets of its Hodge–Tate weights have the same mean.

Varieties and the automorphic side. A PEL datum with trivial division algebra, signature `(1, a−1)` at one embedding and
definite at the others, gives a compact unitary Shimura variety; the `k`-fold fibre power of the universal abelian
scheme is smooth projective, with finite étale Hecke correspondences. The Atiyah–Bott formula for such a correspondence,
for the differential forms of each degree, is proved in the source and applied there. The fixed points are enumerated by
rational elements; their local terms give a Weyl character times `Σ_i y^{h_i}`, and the total is the trace of the Hecke
operator on algebraic modular forms of the definite group. So the eigensystem of `U`, twisted by an algebraic Hecke
character, occurs in `H^b(X, Ω^{h_i})`, `X` the fibre power, for some `b` and each of the `a` expected Hodge degrees
`h_i`. `ρ` is the semisimplification of the joint eigenspace in the total `ℓ`-adic cohomology; its dimension is not
controlled.

Congruence relation. At a split place `w ∤ ℓ` whose residue characteristic splits in `ℚ(√−d)`, every generic point of
the special fibre of the smooth projective model is ordinary at `w` (a Kodaira–Spencer argument and `ker V = im F` on
`H¹_dR`, through an identification of de Rham classes with rigidified extensions of the `p`-divisible group, of
Mazur–Messing type). There the reductions of the quotients of the abelian variety `A` by `j`-dimensional subgroups of
`A[w]` are read off from whether they contain the multiplicative line, and a count on Grassmannians gives pointwise
`Σ_j (−1)^j q^{j(j−1)/2} T_j∘Frob^{a−j} = 0` (`T_j` the Hecke correspondence given by these quotients). Density of
generic points and smooth proper base change make this an operator identity: Frobenius on `ρ` is annihilated by the
Hecke polynomial. At `w | ℓ` of good reduction, `ρ` is crystalline, its Hodge–Tate weights contain the `a` degrees
above, and crystalline Frobenius is killed by a power of the Hecke polynomial.

Cutting out rank `a`. If a semisimple `ρ` is annihilated on a dense set by monic polynomials of degree `a` whose
constant terms come from a character `ψ`, and the Lie algebra of the Zariski closure of its image contains an element
with `a` distinct lacunary eigenvalues (here Sen's operator), then there is `r` of rank `a` with `r ⊂ ρ ⊂ r^{⊕k}` and
`det r = ψ`; at a point of the dense set, `r` has the given polynomial as characteristic polynomial if that
characteristic polynomial is separable, or if the given polynomial is product-generic (separable, and no other multiset
of `a` of its roots has the same product).

Other cases. Odd rank, all weights: `ℓ`-adic congruences on the definite group move the weight at `v′` to a lacunary one
and insert the type at `w₀`, then a limit of traces, crystallinity passing to the limit. Even rank: two theta lifts from
`U(n)` to a definite `U(n+t)`, `n+t` odd, with Satake parameters those of `U` twisted, together with `t` Hecke
characters, the two sets of characters disjoint; the common part is the one wanted. `GL_a`, all CM fields: descent of a
conjugate self-dual `π` to the definite group (B02), cyclic base change of prime degree (B03) and patching; the ramified
clauses come from base changes making the local component an unramified principal series. Definite unitary groups: weak
base change to an isobaric sum of Speh blocks and the representations of its cuspidal constituents.

## Key steps

- **Holomorphic Lefschetz against the definite trace** (`GlobalTheory.T.lefschetz_fixedPoint_finite_etale_correspondence`):
  the Atiyah–Bott formula for finite étale correspondences, proved in the source, summed over fixed points and compared
  with the trace on forms of the definite group (`trace_definite_forms_identity`). The only step tying the definite
  group to the Shimura variety; its proof was not read.
- **Ordinary generic points** (`UpperTheory.Gal.Ord.ordGen`): generic points of the special fibre at suitable split
  places are ordinary; with a Grassmannian count this gives the congruence relation pointwise, then on cohomology
  (`congKS_link`, `esStep_holds`). Read in part.
- **Cutting out rank `a`** (`PositivityTheory.AutomorphicGaloisHodgeTate.Div.DivWCoreLac`): annihilating polynomials on
  a dense set, a determinant character and an element of the Lie algebra with `a` distinct lacunary eigenvalues give
  `r ⊂ ρ ⊂ r^{⊕k}` of rank `a`. Proved as `LateralTheory.DivDb.goal_holds`, whose proof was not read.
- **Congruences and limits** (`GlobalTheory.CongL.CongFinOdd`, a statement, proved by
  `UpperTheory.Gal.Int.Cong.congFinOdd_of_ex` from `UpperTheory.Gal.Wt.wtLacEx_holds`): move the weight to a lacunary
  one and insert the type at `ℓ`, then pass to a limit of traces; crystallinity is closed under the limit
  (`GlobalTheory.LimitTop.closedAtL`).
- **Even rank** (`UpperTheory.TP.TLiftK`, the statement that the theta lift exists, proved as
  `UpperTheory.TP.K.tLiftK`): two theta lifts to definite groups of odd rank with disjoint auxiliary characters, and
  subtraction of the common part.
- **Definite unitary groups** (`UpperTheory.GalN.galoisRep_definiteUnitary_alt`): from weak base change to Speh
  blocks, as in Guerberoff's Theorem 2.3, for every `ℓ` and with crystallinity where `Π` is unramified. Read in part.

## What was checked, and what was not

The first reading read the statements and assembly files of every step and some proofs (`Plan.pack`, `Pack.rep`,
`divSet_of`, `divOne_of`, `rankV`; in part `limWU4_of`, `ordGen`, the definite-group theorem), not the deep proofs: the
`p`-adic comparison theorems (some 450 files), the Lefschetz formula, base change and descent, the theta lifts, the
rank-extraction lemma, the passage from pointwise identities to cohomology. Some definitions in the statement were not
unfolded. Nothing was compiled; a text scan found no `axiom` and no `sorry` outside `Challenge.lean`. Not opened by the
first reading: Kottwitz, Clozel, Harris–Taylor, Clozel–Harris–Labesse, Chenevier–Harris, Labesse, Wedhorn, Moonen,
Fayad–Nekovář, Blasius–Rogawski, Atiyah–Bott, Mazur–Messing; the rest, Scholze–Shin (arXiv:1110.0232) included, was
consulted only through abstracts, introductions or single sections, in summary form. Named by the second opinion from
memory, not checked: Deligne, Faltings–Chai, Wedhorn, Sen, R. Taylor (1993).
