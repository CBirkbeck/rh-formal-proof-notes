## Statement

There is a real constant `C` such that for every prime `ℓ`, every totally complex number field `L` containing a
primitive `ℓ`-th root of unity (a primitive fourth root of unity if `ℓ = 2`) and every integer `E ≥ 1`, the group
`H² = H²(G_{L,ℓ}, ℤ_ℓ(E+1))` is finite and `log #H² ≤ exp((2En)^C · log(2|d_L|))`, where `n = [L:ℚ]` and `d_L` is the
discriminant of `L`.

Here `G_{L,ℓ}` is the Galois group of the maximal extension of `L` unramified at every finite place not above `ℓ`,
`ℤ_ℓ(E+1)` is `ℤ_ℓ` with the `(E+1)`-st power of the cyclotomic character, and the cohomology is that of continuous
cochains. This group is usually written `H²(𝒪_L[1/ℓ], ℤ_ℓ(E+1))`, as étale cohomology; the formal statement does not
include that identification. The roots of unity force `ℓ ≤ n + 1`.

The Lean theorem has four hypotheses, each a named proposition: a rank bound in the homology of the groups `GL_n(𝒪_K)`
(`BOdd`, below), Poitou–Tate duality for finite modules, and finiteness and Tate duality for the cohomology of finite
modules over finite extensions of `ℚ_ℓ`. The source has a theorem without hypotheses for three of them; for
Poitou–Tate duality it has one for a normalised form and a theorem deducing the hypothesis from that form. Only the
existence of `C` is stated; the constants chosen in the proofs give `C = 150`.

## Why it might matter

Once identified with étale cohomology of `𝒪_L[1/ℓ]`, an identification the source does not make, the group is the
target of the `ℓ`-adic Chern class on `K_{2E}(𝒪_L)`; no K-group occurs in the formal statement. The statement bounds
its order in terms of the twist, the degree and the discriminant alone, with `log log #H² ≤ (2En)^C · log(2|d_L|)`, at
the primes `ℓ` with `μ_ℓ ⊂ L`, hence `ℓ ≤ [L:ℚ] + 1`. The proof bounds the length of the twisted Tate–Shafarevich
group `Ш¹(L, ℓ, E)` in the same way. Within the formal proof the proposition is an input to later estimates: about
fifty files refer to it.

## What the checks found

The first reading compared an earlier description: the bound for the étale group `H²(𝒪_L[1/ℓ], ℤ_ℓ(E+1))`, plus a
corestriction form for all fields and all primes. The statement here is about Galois cohomology, is an implication from
four named hypotheses and has no corestriction form, so the comparisons made at all primes do not apply to it; the
others apply to it through the identification with étale cohomology, which the source does not make. Its verdict,
incomparable with medium confidence: Soulé (2003, Theorem 3), Bayer-Fluckiger, Emery and Houriet (2015, Theorem 1.1)
and Emery (2014, `E = 1`), the last two being bounds for K-groups, of which the étale `H²` is a quotient after
tensoring with `ℤ_ℓ`, set aside the small primes, where this statement lives (`ℓ ≤ n + 1`, `ℓ = 2` included), but are
uniform in `ℓ` and bound the product over `ℓ`; they are explicit (except Emery's constant), and of the same shape
(Bayer-Fluckiger, Emery and Houriet) or smaller (Emery, for `E = 1`), while Soulé's bound is much larger in the
discriminant, except for imaginary quadratic fields and `ℚ`. Its own view, not from a source: keeping the small primes
in Soulé's method looks within reach. Its search for later citing works was partial: the citation lists of Soulé
(1999) and of Bayer-Fluckiger, Emery and Houriet were read; that of Soulé (2003) could not be retrieved. The second
opinion agrees, incomparable with medium confidence: the general bounds it checked omit these primes; the statement
gives neither their uniform product bounds nor vanishing; it found no published statement implying the bound and does
not take that as establishing priority, especially for the method; finiteness is classical. Its corrections: drop the
corestriction form; Galois versus étale notation is immaterial here; the prime cutoff error in Soulé (2003) found by
de la Torre Durán is real (`n(4E+1) + 1` is safe); "every published bound excludes these primes" holds only for the
general estimates cited; the quantity bounded by Bayer-Fluckiger et al. is not literally a localization; Emery's gains
are asymptotic, degree and twist fixed. Open: does any published bound, or J. Houriet's 2010 thesis (not opened),
cover these primes?

  `H²(𝒪_L[1/ℓ], ℤ_ℓ(E+1))` over the odd primes `ℓ ≥ 4E + 3` with `ℓ ≥ n + 2` is bounded, through the torsion of
  `K_{2E}(𝒪_L)`; by Proposition 4 the `log log` of the bound grows like `√|d_L| (log|d_L|)^{n−1}` in general and is

  `ℓ`. Its prime cutoff rests on Lemma 9, which de la Torre Durán corrects. Read by the first reading (publisher's

## How the formal proof goes

Write `q = ℓ^ν`, `κ = 1 + ℓ^c`, and `r₂(K)`, `h_K` for the number of complex places and the class number of `K`. The
theorem applies `lemmaK_of_steps` to twenty-one propositions stated in `LemmaKOfSteps.lean`, each proved separately.

**From H² to Ш¹** (`sFinal`). Let `Ш¹(L, ℓ, E)` be the group of classes in `H¹(G_{L,ℓ}, ℚ_ℓ/ℤ_ℓ(−E))` that vanish on a
decomposition group at each place above `ℓ`. The three duality hypotheses, used with finite coefficients and passed to
the limit, give `length H² ≤ length Ш¹ + Σ_{v|ℓ} length H⁰(L_v, ℚ_ℓ/ℤ_ℓ(−E))`. There are at most `n` places `v` and
each local term is at most `2 + log_ℓ n + E`; as `ℓ ≤ n + 1`, it is enough to bound `length Ш¹(L, ℓ, E) · log ℓ` in
the same shape. The duality hypotheses are used nowhere else.

**A larger field** (`SLayer`). Replace `L` by the layer `L′` of degree `ℓ^m` of its cyclotomic `ℤ_ℓ`-extension, with
`4E + 4 ≤ ℓ^m ≤ ℓ(4E + 4)`. If `L′` has `ℓ^c` roots of unity of `ℓ`-power order, then `2E + 1 < ℓ^c ≤ 2[L′:ℚ]`,
`r₂(L′) > 2E + 1`, `|d_{L′}| ≤ |d_L|^{[L′:L]} · (ℓ^c)^{[L′:ℚ]}`, and `length Ш¹(L) ≤ length Ш¹(L′)` by a descent
formula: the two lengths are those of the cokernels of `g − 1` and `g^{ℓ^m} − 1` for one operator `g`.

**Classes as eigen-homomorphisms** (`sSha`). Restriction to `L′(μ_{ℓ^∞})` is injective on `Ш¹(L′, ℓ, E)`, and a class
in the image lives on a layer `F = L′(μ_q)`, where it is a function `φ` from the primes of `F` to `ℤ/q` that vanishes
on principal divisors and satisfies `φ(γ⁻¹P) = κ^E φ(P)`; here `γ` generates `Gal(F/L′)` and acts on `μ_q` by
`ζ ↦ ζ^κ`. So a bound for the number of such `φ`, uniform in `ν`, bounds `#Ш¹(L′, ℓ, E)`.

**Counting through twisted norms** (`sCount`). Let `W` be the group of `α ∈ F^×` with divisor in `(γ⁻¹ − κ^E)Div(F)`,
modulo `(γ⁻¹ − κ^E)F^×` and roots of unity. Its order is that of `Cl(F)/(γ⁻¹ − κ^E)`, whose `ℓ`-part bounds the number
of `φ`, times that of the same quotient of the units, which is `(κ^{E[F:L′]} − 1)^{r₂(L′)}/(κ^E − 1)` because `L′` is
totally complex. With the twisted norm `N(α) = ∏_j γ^j(α)^{κ^{Ej}}` this gives `#{φ} · q^{r₂(L′)} ≤ B · ℓ^X`: `B` is
the number of classes of the `N(α)^{ℓ^b}` modulo `q`-th powers, and
`X = (b + v_ℓ(E))·(2[L′:ℚ] + log₂ h_{L′} + 2) + c + v_ℓ(E)` does not depend on `ν`.

**Twisted norms and the homology of GL** (`sFewOf`). For `b = 2E + (2E+1)(c + 2E + 1)`,
`B ≤ q^{r₂(L′)} · t_{2E+1} · t_{2E}`, with `t_m` the order of the torsion of `H_m(GL_{4E+6}(𝒪_{L′}); ℤ)`. `STheta`:
over a finite field `k ⊃ μ_q`, for `v : k^× → ℤ/q`, an additive class: functionals `θ_V` on the `(2E+1)`-chains modulo
`q` of the bar complex of `GL(V)`, for all `V` of finite dimension, zero on boundaries, invariant under isomorphisms,
additive on direct sums, with value `x^E · v(det g)` on the prism over `g` of a fixed `2E`-cycle of `ℤ/q` acting by
the scalars `ζ^x`. `SClass`: for each `α` a cycle `y_α` of degree `2E + 1` of some `GL_N(𝒪_{L′})` modulo `q` on whose
reduction at each prime `P` of `F` of degree one over `L′`, outside a finite set, `θ` has the value
`pe · v(N(α) mod P)`; `pe = ∏_{j ≤ 2E, j ≠ E} (κ^E − κ^j)` divides a fixed power of `ℓ`. `SPhi`: `θ` composed with
reduction is a primitive family `Φ = (Φ_n)` on the chains of the groups `GL_n(𝒪_{L′})`: zero on boundaries, compatible
with stabilization, and `Φ_{dn} ∘ ψ^d = d · Φ_n` on cycles, `ψ^d(g) = 1_d ⊗ g`. Take `v` with kernel the `q`-th
powers. If `ℓ^{2E} Φ` agrees on `y_α` and `y_{α′}` at every `P`, then `N(α)^{ℓ^b}/N(α′)^{ℓ^b}` is a `q`-th power
modulo every `P`, hence in `F` (`SDetect`, by the density of Frobenius elements at primes of degree one). So `B` is at
most the number of values on cycles of `ℓ^{2E} Φ`, for one primitive family with values in a power of `ℤ/q`.

**Counting values** (`sUpperOf`). A Bockstein argument passes to integral classes at the cost of the factor `t_{2E}`.
On `H_{2E+1}(GL_n(𝒪_{L′}); ℤ)`, `ℓ^{2E} Φ(x) = u · Φ(z(x))` with `z(x) = ∏_{t<2E} (ψ^ℓ − ℓ^{t+2})(x)`, and by `BOdd`
the `z(x)` span a group of rank at most `r₂(L′)`. A group of rank `r` with torsion of order `t` has at most `q^r · t`
images in a group killed by `q`, and homological stability gives the same torsion for all `n ≥ 4E + 6`. The source
proves `BOdd` as `bOdd`; to judge from the statements of its steps (the proofs were not read), through invariant
differential forms sorted by weight under `ψ^d`.

**Torsion and assembly** (`sTors`, `sNum`). For totally complex `K`, `N ≤ 4E + 6` and `m ≤ 2E + 1`,
`log #H_m(GL_N(𝒪_K); ℤ)_tors ≤ exp((2E[K:ℚ])^{24} log(2|d_K|))`, every prime included: the group acts on the acyclic
nerve of the cover by perfect Hermitian forms, with counted orbits and stabilizers of order at most `3^{[K:ℚ]N²}`. In
`sNum` the factors `q^{r₂(L′)}` cancel: `ℓ^{length Ш¹(L′)} ≤ t_{2E+1} · t_{2E} · ℓ^X`; the bounds of the second step
and `log h_{L′} = O([L′:ℚ] log(2|d_{L′}|))` then give the bound for `L`. The density of Frobenius elements,
homological stability and the class number bound are theorems of the source.

## Key steps

- **From H² to Ш¹** (`UpperTheory.V.ZK.sFinal`): `length H²` is at most `length Ш¹(L, ℓ, E)` plus local terms
  polynomial in `n` and `E`. The three duality hypotheses enter here and only here, through
  `UpperTheory.V.LDataCardLeFinrank.VLen_le_shaLen_add`.
- **Classes as eigen-homomorphisms** (`PositivityTheory.QuaternionShaBounds.sSha`): a bound, uniform in `ν`, for the
  functions `φ` on the primes of `L′(μ_{ℓ^ν})` with `φ(γ⁻¹P) = κ^E φ(P)` that vanish on principal divisors bounds
  `#Ш¹(L′, ℓ, E)`. The cohomological group becomes a count on class groups.
- **Count through twisted norms** (`UpperTheory.V.ZK.sCount`): `#{φ} · q^{r₂(L′)} ≤ B · ℓ^X` with `X` independent of
  `ν`. The factor `q^{r₂(L′)}` comes from the units of the layer over a totally complex base; the argument works
  because the homological count has the same factor.
- **A cycle with prescribed residues** (`UpperTheory.V.ZK.sClassOf`): for `α` with divisor in `(γ⁻¹ − κ^E)Div(F)`, a
  cycle of `GL_N(𝒪_{L′})` modulo `q` on whose reductions the additive classes take the value `pe · v(N(α) mod P)`; it
  is glued from prisms over a relation among `2E + 2` embeddings of `ℤ/q` in `(ℤ/q)²`.
- **Separation by residues** (`LateralTheory.Q.SFewOfCut.sFewOf`): distinct classes of twisted norms give distinct
  values of one primitive family, hence `B ≤ q^{r₂(L′)} · t_{2E+1} · t_{2E}`. It uses the density of Frobenius
  elements at primes of degree one (`LateralTheory.Q.SDetectCut.goal`).
- **The rank bound** (`UpperTheory.V.ZK.bOdd`): for totally complex `K` and even `k` with `k + 1 < r₂(K)`, the classes
  `∏_{t<k} (ψ^ℓ − ℓ^{t+2})(x)`, `x ∈ H_{k+1}(GL_n(𝒪_K); ℤ)`, `n ≥ 2k + 6`, span a group of rank at most `r₂(K)`. It
  gives the exponent `r₂` in the count of values (`UpperTheory.V.ZK.sUpperOf`).

## What was checked, and what was not

`UpperTheory.V.ZK.lemmaK` is a proved theorem; for its four hypotheses the source has the theorems
`UpperTheory.V.ZK.bOdd`, `PositivityTheory.Lit1.PTN.poitouTateNormalised_holds` (a normalised form, from which
`GlobalTheory.PoitouTateConj.poitouTateDuality_of_normalised` deduces the hypothesis), and `top_locfin`, `top_tate` in
`CompanionTheory.LocalTateOrthogonality.Gcoh`. No declaration with the bound and no hypothesis was found:
`UpperTheory.V.ZK.lemmaK_litN_alt` still assumes a bundle of published theorems that another theorem supplies. Read in
full: the statements, `lemmaK_of_steps`, `sFinal`, `sNum`, `sCount`, `sFewOf`, `sUpperOf`, `torsAll`, the last step of
`sSha`, and the proofs of `SLayer` and `SDetect`; as statements only: all below these, notably the cycle, the additive
classes, `bOdd`, the torsion bound and the duality theorems. A text search of some 9,400 Lean files found no `sorry`
and no `axiom` declaration. Nothing was compiled; `C = 150` was read off three proofs.
