## Statement

The statement is about linear data, not about an abelian scheme.

Let `p` be an odd prime, `C` an algebraically closed field of characteristic `0` with a real-valued valuation such that
`0 < |p| < 1`, `R` its valuation ring (a `ℤ_p`-algebra), and `c_p ∈ R` an element with `|c_p|^{p−1} = |p|`. The data are
the following.

- A commutative `ℤ_p`-algebra `O` with an involution `σ`, acting on `T = ℤ_p^{2g}` and on `Lie = R^g`, hence on
  `ω = Hom(Lie, R)`.
- A unimodular alternating form `E` on `T` with `E(ax, y) = E(x, σ(a)y)`.
- `R`-linear maps `𝔥 : T ⊗ R → ω` and `𝔟 : T ⊗ R → Lie` with `𝔥(ax) = σ(a)𝔥(x)` and
  `⟨𝔟x, 𝔥y⟩ − ⟨𝔟y, 𝔥x⟩ = c_p·E(x, y)`.
- A Hopf order `𝒜`: an `R`-lattice of `R`-valued functions on `T/pT` containing `1`, stable under products, under
  `f ↦ f(−x)` and under `O`, with `f(x + y) ∈ 𝒜 ⊗ 𝒜`. It comes with an `O`-equivariant isomorphism `I/I² ≅ ω/pω`, where
  `I` is the set of functions of `𝒜` vanishing at `0`, and it contains functions congruent modulo `p` to each coordinate
  of `𝔥` and of `𝔟`.
- Two projectors, taken to be zero.

Assume that idempotents split `T ⊗ R` and `Lie` into parts on which `O` acts through ring maps
`τ_1, …, τ_m : O → R`, pairwise distinct modulo the maximal ideal `𝔪` and permuted by `τ ↦ τ∘σ` and by a map `φ` with
`φτ ≡ τ^p` modulo `𝔪`. Assume that `Lie_τ` is free of rank `r(τ)`. Put `ω_τ = Hom(Lie_τ, R)`, and define `x(τ)` by
`|p|^{x(τ)} = ∏_i |c_i|` if `ω_τ/𝔥((T ⊗ R)_{τ∘σ}) ≅ ⊕_i R/(c_i)`. Then, for every `τ`,

`x(τ) − x(τ∘σ) = r(τ)/(p − 1) − Σ_{n≥0} p^{−(n+1)}·r(φ^{n+1}τ)`.

In the Lean source this is a proposition, and the theorem `u7` proves it without further hypotheses.

## Context

The data are modelled on a principally polarised abelian scheme over `R` with an action of the integers `𝒪` of a
number field unramified at `p`, where an involution of `𝒪` is adjoint for the polarisation. In that case `T` is the
Tate module, `O = 𝒪 ⊗ ℤ_p`, `𝔥` is the Hodge–Tate map composed with the polarisation, and `𝒜` is the Hopf algebra of the
`p`-torsion. We did not find in the source a construction of the data from such a scheme, so the relation is proved for
the linear data only. In the geometric case it would say that the degrees of the Hodge–Tate cokernel at `τ` and at
`τ∘σ` differ by a quantity that depends only on the ranks `r(τ)`.

## Comparison with the literature

We compared with the literature a different statement: the relation for a principally polarised abelian scheme with an
action of the integers of a number field unramified at `p`. The Lean statement is about linear data, and we found no
passage from such schemes to such data, so what follows bears on the formal statement only once that passage is made.
In the geometric form, we found the relation in print only near the μ-ordinary locus or where a canonical subgroup
exists. Hernandez's exact cokernel degree (2019), which needs a small partial Hasse invariant and `p` large compared
with the signature, together with his duality of partial Hasse invariants (2018), gives the relation in that range,
though neither paper states this deduction. Hernandez (2022, Theorem 5.6 (5)) gives it for every `p` where a canonical
subgroup exists, and Fargues (2011) at places of parallel signature. So the geometric form would improve on what we
found, but it may be folklore, and our search was limited. We did not read Chen's paper on the determinant morphism
(2013), we made only a brief search of the literature, and of the later literature we looked only at the author's
later papers, where we found nothing beyond that range.

A second, independent check could not decide. In its view no improvement is established, because the Lean statement
neither constructs the geometric data nor proves the canonical-subgroup theorems, and geometric theorems do not apply
automatically to arbitrary Hopf-order data. It found no publication stating the relation for such data. The two
assessments disagree: ours, of an improvement, concerns abelian schemes, and the second, undecided, concerns these
linear data. We would like to know whether abelian schemes with such an action give data satisfying these hypotheses,
and whether Chen (2013), or determinants of Breuil–Kisin–Fargues modules, give the relation at every point.

## Sketch of the proof

The proof is linear algebra over `R` and algebra in `𝒜`. No geometry enters. Let `v` be the valuation with `v(p) = 1`,
and `e_τ` the idempotents. Degrees of finitely presented torsion `R`-modules rest on a Smith normal form over the
valuation ring, proved in the source (`Smith.smith_gen`), with existence (`lenEx`), independence of the presentation
(`hasLen_unique`) and additivity in exact sequences (`hasLen_ext`).

(1) An equivariant second row (`u1`). As the `τ` are distinct modulo `𝔪`, `E` pairs `(T ⊗ R)_τ` with `(T ⊗ R)_{τ∘σ}`
only, and `𝔥` maps `(T ⊗ R)_τ` into `ω_{τ∘σ}` (`εT_E`, `ht_εT`). Replace `𝔟` by `𝔟′ = Σ_τ e_τ𝔟e_τ` (`bt2`). The
relation with `c_p·E` persists, `𝔟′` commutes with the idempotents, and the matrix `Π′` with rows `𝔥`, `𝔟′` has
`(det Π′)² = c_p^{2g}·det E`, so `v(det Π′) = g/(p − 1)`.

(2) Auxiliary colengths. `Π′` maps `(T ⊗ R)_τ` into `ω_{τ∘σ} ⊕ Lie_τ`. Let `ξ(τ)` be the degree of the cokernel
(`xModE`). As `τ` varies these blocks decompose the source and the target of `Π′`, so
`Σ_τ ξ(τ) = v(det Π′) = g/(p − 1)` (`eigenLengthProductStatement_holds`).

(3) Duality (`lenId`). Projection to `ω_{τ∘σ}` presents that cokernel as an extension of `ω_{τ∘σ}/𝔥((T ⊗ R)_τ)`, of
degree `x(τ∘σ)`, by `Lie_τ/N_τ`, where `N_τ` is the set of `y` with `(0, y)` in the image. By the relation with
`c_p·E`, `y ∈ N_τ` exactly when `c_p` divides `𝔥ᵀy`, and `c_p` kills `ω_τ/𝔥((T ⊗ R)_{τ∘σ})` (`exists_fromRows_mulVec_iff`,
`cok_kill`). For a matrix `A` with `d` rows whose image contains `c_p·R^d`, the lattice of `y` with `c_p` dividing `Aᵀy`
has colength `d·v(c_p)` minus that of the image of `A` (`dualLen`). Hence `ξ(τ) = r(τ)/(p − 1) − x(τ) + x(τ∘σ)`, and
the theorem becomes `ξ(τ) = Σ_{n≥0} p^{−(n+1)}·r(φ^{n+1}τ)`.

(4) Lifts in the Hopf order. A Smith basis adapted to the blocks (`elementary_divisor_statement`) gives a unimodular
matrix `Λ` of linear forms on `T ⊗ R`, the row `Λ_j` living on one block `κ(j)`, and elements `ε_j ∈ R` dividing `c_p`,
such that the `ε_jΛ_j` with `κ(j) = τ` span the rows of `Π′e_τ` and `Σ_{κ(j)=τ} v(ε_j) = ξ(τ)`. Since the coordinates of
`𝔥` and `𝔟` are congruent modulo `p` to elements of `𝒜`, so is each `ε_jΛ_j`. The source builds a finite abelian group
`G` of order prime to `p`, acting on `T` through elements of `O`, on which the `τ` are nontrivial characters, pairwise
distinct modulo `𝔪`, with `φτ = τ^p` exactly (`embeddingSeparatingGroup`). Averaging over `G` against `κ(j)` gives
`x_j = ε_ju_j ∈ I` on which `G` acts through `κ(j)`, with `u_j ≡ Λ_j` modulo `𝔪` (`lift_one`). Here `p ≠ 2` is used,
since `v(ε_j) ≤ 1/(p − 1) < 1` and so `p/ε_j ∈ 𝔪`, and it is also used in the construction of `G`.

(5) Monomial basis (`monomials_span_of_prod_eq`). The `p^{2g}` monomials `x^α`, `0 ≤ α_j < p`, lie in `𝒜`, and the
determinant of their values has valuation `(p^{2g}(p − 1)/2)·Σ_j v(ε_j) = g·p^{2g}/2`. This is `u5`: modulo `𝔪` the
`u^α` are the monomials in a basis of linear forms on `𝔽_p^{2g}`, which form a basis of all functions. For a Hopf order on
an elementary abelian `p`-group `N`, twice its colength in the `R`-valued functions is at least `|N|` times the degree of
`I/I²` (`u4`, by induction on `|N|` from the case of order `p`, `u3`). Here this is `g·p^{2g}`. So the monomials are an
`R`-basis of `𝒜`, and the `x_j` generate `I` modulo `I²`.

(6) Frobenius inequality (`frob_of`). Expand `x_j^p`, with `κ(j) = τ`, in the monomial basis. `G` acts on it through
`φτ`, so the coefficient of `x_l` vanishes unless `κ(l) = φτ` (`bR_off`). In that case it is `b_{jl}·ε_j^p/ε_l`, where
`b_{jl} ∈ R` reduces modulo `𝔪` to the matrix writing the `p`-th powers of the residual forms `Λ̄_j` in the `Λ̄_l`. That
matrix is invertible block by block (`block_det_ne`, the blocks `τ` and `φτ` having equal size by
`rank_eq_rank_of_perm_and_zero`). So this square matrix `B_τ` has `v(det B_τ) = p·ξ(τ) − ξ(φτ)` (`frobeniusBlock`). Its
rows are linear parts of elements of `I²`, as `x_j^p ∈ I²`. Consider the map sending coefficients `c` on the block `φτ`
to the class of `Σ c_lx_l` in `I/I² ≅ ω/pω`, projected to `ω_{φτ}/p ≅ (R/p)^{r(φτ)}`. It vanishes on such rows, and it
is onto because the `x_l` with `κ(l) ≠ φτ` have zero component there (`phi_x_grade`, from the equivariance of the
isomorphism). So `p·ξ(τ) − ξ(φτ) ≥ r(φτ)` (`u7F3`).

(7) Equality (`unramifiedCokernelLength_of_length_eigenLengthProduct`). Summing over `τ` gives
`(p − 1)·Σ_τ ξ(τ) ≥ Σ_τ r(τ) = g`, and the left side equals `g` by (2). So every inequality in (6) is an equality,
`ξ(τ) = (ξ(φτ) + r(φτ))/p`, and iteration gives the series.

No published theorem enters as an unproved input. The Smith normal form, the facts `u3` and `u4` on Hopf orders and the
determinant `u5` are proved in the same directory, on top of Mathlib. That `C` is algebraically closed is used in `u3`,
for a primitive `p`-th root of unity.

## Key steps

- **Duality between conjugate embeddings** (`lenId`): `src/ReciprocityTheory/DualLattices/MatEquivApply.lean`, line 215,
  with the reduction `lenIdB_of` in `DualLenStatement.lean`, line 91. It gives `ξ(τ) = r(τ)/(p − 1) − x(τ) + x(τ∘σ)`
  from the relation between `𝔥`, `𝔟` and `E`. After it the statement concerns only the auxiliary colengths `ξ`.
- **The total of the auxiliary colengths** (`u1`): `src/ReciprocityTheory/DualLattices/StdJForm.lean`, line 28. After
  averaging `𝔟` over the idempotents, the square of the determinant of the period matrix is `c_p^{2g}·det E`, which fixes
  `Σ_τ ξ(τ) = g/(p − 1)`. This is the global count that later forces equality.
- **Isotypic lifts** (`lift_one`): `src/ReciprocityTheory/DualLattices/AvgFZero.lean`, line 225. Each form `ε_jΛ_j` of a
  Smith basis is lifted to an element of `𝒜` vanishing at `0`, isotypic for a group of order prime to `p`, and equal to
  `ε_j` times a function reducing to `Λ_j`. The hypothesis `p ≠ 2` is used here and in building that group.
- **Monomial basis of the Hopf order** (`monomials_span_of_prod_eq`):
  `src/ReciprocityTheory/DualLattices/MonomialsSpanOfProdEq.lean`, line 77. The monomials in the lifts span a lattice
  whose colength meets the lower bound `u4`, valid for every Hopf order, so they span `𝒜` and the lifts generate `I/I²`.
- **Frobenius inequality** (`u7F3`): `src/ReciprocityTheory/DualLattices/SumX.lean`, line 11, with `frobeniusBlock` in
  `FrobeniusBlock.lean`, line 12. The linear parts of the `x_j^p` are relations in `I/I² ≅ ω/pω`, whose `φτ`-part is
  free of rank `r(φτ)` over `R/p`. This gives `p·ξ(τ) − ξ(φτ) ≥ r(φτ)`, and it is where the rank at `φτ` enters.
- **Equality from the count** (`unramifiedCokernelLength_of_length_eigenLengthProduct`):
  `src/ReciprocityTheory/DualLattices/UnramifiedCokernelLengthOfLengthEigenLengthProduct.lean`, line 208. The
  inequalities sum to an equality, so each is one, and the recursion `ξ(τ) = (ξ(φτ) + r(φτ))/p` is solved by the series.

## Caveats

`UnramifiedCokernelLengthStatement` is the definition of a proposition, and `u7` is a theorem of that type, without
hypotheses, composed of closed theorems, one for each intermediate proposition. We read in full step (7), `u1`, the
reductions `lenIdB_of` and `xModE_len` of step (3), `lifts_of`, `lift_one`, `embeddingSeparatingGroup`,
`monomials_span_of_prod_eq`, `frob_of`, `frobeniusBlock` and `u7F3`. We read only in part, or as statements, the Smith
normal form, `dualLen`, `eigenLengthProductStatement_holds`, `u3`, `u4`, `u5`, the block Smith basis and the elements
behind `G`. A text search of the 96 project files imported, directly or not, by the file of `u7` found neither `sorry`
nor `axiom`, and beyond them only Mathlib is imported. Nothing was compiled. We have not checked that an abelian scheme
as above gives data satisfying the hypotheses. The source has statements in that direction, which we saw only as
statements: a datum with `O = ℤ_p` and trivial `σ` from a principally polarised abelian scheme over a `p`-adically
complete `R`, and the idempotents and characters from a ring of integers unramified at `p` once a datum with its action
is given. We found none that produces the datum with the action.
