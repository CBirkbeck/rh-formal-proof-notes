## Statement

For every real `C` there is `n₀` (not given; the proof takes about `exp(1424 + 352·C)`) such that the following holds.
Let `E/F` be a CM extension unramified at every finite place, `f = [F:ℚ]`, `rd_E = |d_E|^{1/(2f)}`; `J` a nondegenerate
Hermitian matrix of size `n ≥ n₀` over `E` with an `𝒪_E`-lattice `L ⊂ Eⁿ` equal to its dual, of one signature `(p, q)`
at all real places (`q` negative directions), `q² ≤ n`, `log rd_E ≤ eⁿ`, `log f ≤ √n`; `G = U(J)`; `b ≥ 2` with
`log b ≤ exp(n·f·log n/16)`. At each real place `v` let `σ_v` be a discrete series of `G(F_v)` with Harish-Chandra
parameter `λ_v` (encoded in the Lean through its minimal `K`-type `λ_v + ρ_n − ρ_c`), with `|λ_{v,a}| ≤ b²` and
`|λ_{v,a} − λ_{v,a′}| ≥ 8·10¹⁴·(C+4)·n¹⁹·f⁵·log(b·rd_E)` when the directions `a`, `a′` have different signs for the form
at `v` (one among the `p` positive, one among the `q` negative). Let `e` be a locally constant function on `G(𝔸_f)` with
`e ∗ e = e`, `e(x⁻¹) = conj e(x)`, of positive type, supported in the stabiliser of the adelic completion `L̂` of `L`,
bi-invariant under its principal congruence subgroup of a level `g`, `1 ≤ g ≤ b^C`, and supported on `x` with
`(x − 1)ⁿL̂ ⊂ g₀L̂`, `g₀ > 4n` a rational prime split completely in `E`.

Let `m` be the dimension of the space of maps `R(e)∘T`, `T` a continuous `G(F_∞)`-intertwiner from `⊗_v σ_v` to
`L²(G(F)\G(𝔸))`. Then `m` is finite and `|m − D*| ≤ exp(−n·f·log n/16)·D*`, `D* = ∏_v d_v·vol(G(F)\G(𝔸))·e(1)`, with
`d_v` the formal degree of `σ_v`, all for the same Haar measures.

## Why it might matter

Limit multiplicity compares the multiplicity of a discrete series in `L²` of an arithmetic quotient with formal
degree times volume. Here the comparison is one inequality with an explicit relative error, for all ranks above a
threshold and, together, all CM extensions unramified at every finite place, levels and weights in the stated ranges,
on quotients that need not be compact. For a fixed group the error is a fixed number: it does not tend to zero as the level or the weight grows.

## What the checks found

The first reading compared a wider statement: any CM extension, `n₀` not given, the cuspidal multiplicity. The
statement here needs `E/F` unramified at every finite place, has `n₀` about `exp(1424 + 352C)` in the proof, counts in
all of `L²` (cuspidality proved inside), and is reached only by composing three proved theorems. Its fixed-group
comparisons stand; the uniformity it credits holds here only over such extensions, above that threshold. Its verdict,
incomparable with medium confidence: Shin–Templier, Dalal, Dalal–Gerbelli-Gauthier and Kim–Wakatsuki–Yamauchi fix the
group and get errors tending to `0` with level or weight, which the fixed error here does not; Savin has no rate; no
rank-uniform bound was found, and field uniformity only for groups of type `A₁` (Matz, Frączyk). Six papers were read
only through summaries, some classical ones not at all. No later forward search was made. The second opinion agrees,
incomparable with medium confidence: the closest theorems leave the dependence on the rank uncontrolled; not a
restatement, but sharply limited by threshold, signatures, self-duality and auxiliary support. Corrections: "any CM
extension" must read "unramified at every finite place"; Dalal's weight measures distance from all root walls, here
only opposite signs are separated; for a discrete series the extra parity tests of Dalal–Gerbelli-Gauthier disappear;
abstracts do not certify every quoted rate. Only the second opinion calls the case `q = 0` classical in substance.
Open: does any published bound control the dependence on the rank? Where is the case `q = 0` in print?

## How the formal proof goes

The statement is a definition of a proposition. The source proves it from one hypothesis, the volume step
(`countingTheorem_of_volumeStep`), proves the volume step from the Siegel–Weil formula for unitary groups in the
convergent range, and proves that formula separately. The estimate is assembled in `countingTheorem_of_finite_of_bounds`.

Projector and trace. Put `φ = (∏_v d_v·conj⟨w_v, σ_v(·)w_v⟩) ⊗ e`, `w_v` a unit vector in the lowest `K`-type. Under a
gap `h` the coefficient is at most `exp(−(h/8)·min(t², t/√(2q)))`, `t` the Hilbert–Schmidt size of the Cartan component
(proved in the source), so `φ` is integrable. By `globalProj_holds`, which rests on Schur orthogonality, `R(φ)` is
an orthogonal projector whose range corresponds to the space counted by `m` through `T ↦ T(⊗w_v)`. `kernelB` gives
an orthonormal basis `F_1, …, F_m` of the range by continuous automorphic functions with
`K(x, y) = Σ_γ φ(x⁻¹γy) = Σ_i F_i(x)·conj F_i(y)` (`γ ∈ G(F)`) and `∫ K(x, x) dx = m` over a fundamental domain; the term
`γ = 1` integrates to `D*`.

Near terms. For `x ∈ G(𝔸)` let `Λ_x` be the lattice `x_f L` with the metrics given by `x_∞` and `least(x)` the least
normalised covolume of a nonzero sublattice; the moderate part is `least(x) ≥ τ* = (n·f·rd_E·g·b)^{−11q}`, the deep
part the rest. Call `γ` near if `x⁻¹γx` is within Cartan distance `ε = 10⁻⁶·n⁻⁸·f⁻²` of the maximal compact subgroup at
every real place. By `near_unipotent`, for a near `γ` with `φ(x⁻¹γx) ≠ 0` the traces `tr γ^j`, `j ≤ n`, are within `3n` of
`n` in every complex embedding and congruent to `n` modulo `g₀𝒪_E`, a radical ideal; as `g₀ > 3n` they equal `n`, so `γ`
is unipotent and `x⁻¹γx` is within `δ = 3√(n·ε)` of `1`. If `least(x) ≥ 1/2` this forces `γ = 1`. Otherwise `x` is sorted
by the type of its canonical flag: the steps of the canonical filtration of `Λ_x` of size below `1/2`, which are
isotropic, of ranks `j_1, …, j_a` (`J = Σ j_i ≤ q`) and sizes `τ_1 < … < τ_a`. `near_count` bounds the number of near
elements by a product of factors `1 + 12δ/λ` over ratios `λ` of the sizes `τ_i, 1, 1/τ_i` of the self-dual chain.
`flagMeasureBound` bounds the measure of the points of one type with sizes in `B` by `vol·κ·∫_B (density)`: it unfolds
along the parabolic subgroup of the flag (`unfoldBound`), evaluates volumes for `GL_j` over `E` by the residue and
values of `ζ_E`, and uses the ratio of the volumes for `U(p, q)` and `U(p − J, q − J)` given by the volume step.
`constantBound` gives `κ ≤ exp(−J·n·f·log n/2)` for `log n ≥ 15`. Summed over fewer than `2^q` types the near terms
contribute at most `exp(−n·f·log n/4)·D*` (`nearModerate`); a power of `log(1/τ*)` is absorbed here, which is where
`n₀` depends on `C`.

Far terms. `farBound_of_ptBound` sums the decay of the coefficient over dyadic shells against the lattice-point
count `#{γ : distance ≤ r} ≤ (3√n/τ₁(x)²)^{2n²f}·exp(2n²·Σ_v r_v)`, `τ₁ = min(1/2, least)`. On the moderate part the
size of the gap outweighs the count and the far terms contribute at most `exp(−n²f)·D*` (`farModerate_of_farBound`).

Deep part. Here `K(x, x) = Σ_i |F_i(x)|²` is bounded directly; the `F_i` are values of isometric intertwiners with
cuspidal images. By `unipotent_orbit_cover` there is a step `W` of the canonical flag such that every point of
`N_W(F_∞)x` is, up to `G(F)` on the left and the principal congruence subgroup on the right, a point `x·exp(A)·exp(Z)`
of that orbit with `‖A‖ + ‖Z‖ ≤ ρ = n²·√f·g·rd_E·(2·least(x))^{1/q}/4` (a covering-radius estimate proved in the source;
by its imports it goes back to a transference bound, also proved there). As the constant term along `N_W` vanishes,
`step_bound` bounds the values near `x` by `ρ` times a bound for derivatives along `Lie N_W`, one step further from the
lowest `K`-type; this is iterated `8q·n²f` times and closed by a Sobolev bound. With `vol ≤ (n·f·rd_E·g·b)^{n²f}` in the
Hilbert–Schmidt normalisation, from the volume step, this gives `∫_deep K(x, x) dx ≤ exp(−n²f)·m` (`deepBound`).

Assembly. `nonzeroFlagBound` shows `least(x) < 1/2` only on a set of measure at most `exp(−n·f·log n/3)·vol`, which
bounds the identity term missing on the deep part. Together
`|m − D*| ≤ (2·exp(−n·f·log n/4) + exp(−n²f))·D* + exp(−n²f)·m`, which gives the stated bound for `n ≥ e¹⁵`. For `q = 0`
every contributing `γ` is unipotent, hence trivial, and `m = D*` exactly. Finiteness of `m` is proved first, from
pointwise bounds of the same kind and the finiteness of the volume (`rows_finiteDimensional`).

Volume step. For `n ≥ 3` the volumes, in the Hilbert–Schmidt normalisation, of the quotients for `J` and for the
orthogonal complement of a vector of norm `±1` have the ratio `rd_E^{f(n−1/2)}·L(n, ηⁿ)`, `η` the character of `E/F`.
`volumeStep_of_siegelMean` gets this by integrating a sum of a test function over the lattice vectors of that norm
in two ways: by Siegel's mean value formula (the one-vector case of the Siegel–Weil formula) and by unfolding to
the stabiliser of a vector.

## What was checked, and what was not
