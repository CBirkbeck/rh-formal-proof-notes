## Statement

Data of the proof: a CM field `E` (a special abelian one), a prime `l` (`2` allowed), a DVR `O₀` finite over `ℤ_l`, odd
`r ≥ 100`, odd `t ≥ r + 5`, finitely many `T_s = ρ_s ⊗ χ_s⁻¹`, `ρ_s : G_E → GL_r(O₀)` continuous. For each `s`:
`ρ̄_s(G_E)` spans the matrix algebra; `T_s^†∘c ≅ T_s ⊗ χ_cyc^t` (`†`: Tate dual, `c`: complex conjugation); `ρ_s` is
unramified outside `l` and two primes `g_i`, and at `g_i ≠ l` wild inertia is trivial on `ρ̄_s` and an inertia element
has no eigenvalue `1` on `T̄_s`; above `l`, `T_s` is de Rham, potentially crystalline, without Hodge–Tate jump in
`[−1, t]`, and `dim D_dR/Fil⁰` at `τ` and `τ∘c` add up to `r`; no decomposition group fixes a vector `≠ 0` of `T_s[1/l]`
or its Tate dual; `χ_s = δ^{±1}` on inertia above a third prime, `δ` of order `15` there (so an inertia element `z` acts
on each `T̄_s` by a scalar `ϑ_s`, `ϑ_s² ≠ 1`). Then for all `ε′ > 0` and finite sets `A` of places there are a finite
set `Q` of degree-one places, split over `E⁺`, outside `A`, and a character `ψ` of `G_E` of `l`-power order,
anticyclotomic, trivial on decomposition groups above `l`, ramified exactly at `Q`, with
`length Sel_Q(T_s ⊗ ψ^a ⊗ E′/O′) ≤ (ν + ε_s)·length(O′/l)` over a DVR `O′ ⊇ O₀` with fraction field `E′`, for all `s`
and all `a` prime to `l`; here `Σ_s ε_s ≤ ε′`, `ν` maximal with `χ_cyc^{tr} ≡ 1 (mod l^ν)`. `Sel_Q`: Bloch–Kato
condition above `l`, none at `Q`, the `g_i` and the third prime, unramified elsewhere. More below.

## Why it might matter

The statement bears on how short the Selmer groups of twists of a conjugate self-dual lattice can be made by one tamely
ramified character of `l`-power order; it is derived, from four named propositions, for the special CM field and
lattices of the formal proof, not for an arbitrary CM field. The bound is obtained from Galois cohomology and class
field theory, without L-values or Euler systems; it covers `l = 2`, several lattices at once and all prime-to-`l` powers
of the character. In the formal proof the same character must also satisfy conditions on automorphic objects.

## What the checks found

The first reading compared a wider statement (any CM field; abstract lattices, local condition above `l` and scalar
element; at most `4R` ramified places). The statement here concerns the proof's own data (a special abelian CM field,
odd `r ≥ 100`, odd `t ≥ r + 5`, three auxiliary primes), comes from four named propositions and does not bound the
ramified places; it lies inside the range compared, and the first reading's searches aimed at the wider statement. The
verdict of the first reading, incomparable with medium confidence: Mazur and Rubin get vanishing twisted Selmer groups
for abelian varieties, but at `t = 0`, excluded here, and with an element of one-dimensional coinvariants; a preprint of
October 2026 uses the same device in rank `2` at `t = 0`. Some of the searches it planned were not run; some comparisons
rest on abstracts. A later forward search, made with the same wider statement, left this unchanged: nothing it found
(nearest: Smith, Assim–Movahhedi, Kriz–Nordentoft) implies that statement or follows from it. The second opinion agrees,
incomparable with medium confidence, and classes it as a technical lemma limited by its fields, ranks, weights,
ramification and named hypotheses. Corrections: "any rank", "any CM field" and "only irreducibility plus a scalar"
misdescribe this statement; vanishing of a strict Selmer group does not bound the larger relaxed one, so Mazur–Rubin is
not simply "stronger"; small normalised length does not imply vanishing; the analogy between `ν` and a root-number
exception is unsupported; it did not verify the preprint. Open: is such a twist published for conjugate self-dual
lattices of higher rank with `t ≠ 0`? Can Smith's conditions hold for them?

## How the formal proof goes

The proposition concluded is `admissibleIndexBoundAllPowers`; the main theorem derives it from four hypotheses that are
named propositions: the Galois-side statement below; `AdmACD` (the properties of the `T_s`, Bloch–Kato duality and
dimension statements for the period rings, statements on automorphic objects); and `DelUTie`, `PropABCD`, linking
characters and polynomials to the automorphic side. The `s` are those marked 'good' there; `g_i ≥ 2r` and an index
condition are assumed, and, for the automorphic clauses, two conditions on group-ring elements and a condition (`ABHyp`)
on a family of lattices of forms; above `l`, `ρ_s` satisfies the same conditions as `T_s`, and both are crystalline if
`l ≠ g_i`. The conclusion also has `t·Σ_{P∈Q} v_l(ψ(σ_P) − 1) ≤ ε′` (`σ_P` an inertia element at `P` with `ψ(σ_P) ≠ 1`,
of least valuation) and two clauses on automorphic objects.

Galois side (`galois_twisted_selmer_auxprimes_of_matrix_and_bounds`). For finitely many `T_s` there are `R`, `D`, `M₀`
such that at every level `M ≥ M₀` and every finite `A` there are `R` first-half and `R` second-half places avoiding `A`,
and per `s` a polynomial `F` over `O₀` in `2R` variables with
`length Sel_Q(T ⊗ ψ_u⁻¹ ⊗ E′/O′)/length(O′/l) = v_l(F(u − 1))` for all `u ∈ μ_{l^M}^{2R}`, `ψ_u = ∏ u_q^{d_q}`, and a
coefficient of valuation `≤ v(l^ν)` in degree `≤ D`. The `d_q : G_E → ℤ/l^M` are anticyclotomic, trivial above `l`,
ramified exactly at `q` and `cq` (class field theory). `F` lifts `det Φ`, where `Φ` is a square matrix over
`Λ = O₀[(ℤ/l^M)^{2R}]` with `Sel_Q(T ⊗ κ⁻¹ ⊗ B) ≅ ker(Φ | B^a)` naturally in torsion `B`, `κ = ∏[d_q]`; it exists since
this functor is left exact with bounded defect of exactness (Poitou–Tate, Euler characteristics, the `Fil⁰` condition).
Places have norm `≡ 1 (mod 2l^M)`. First-half Frobenius elements are conjugate to `z` modulo an open normal subgroup `L`
(stable under `c`, trivial on `T̄_s`, `z` central modulo it), so local cohomology with torsion coefficients vanishes
there; second-half decomposition groups act trivially on `T̄_s`, and `d_b(Frob_a) = δ_ab` across the halves.

Frobenius classes (`chooseH`). A cocycle vanishing on `L` is a coboundary (`z` is a scalar `≠ 1`, central modulo `L`);
so classes in `S = Sel_∅(T̄_s)` and `S′ = Sel_∅(T̄_s^†)` are equivariant homomorphisms on `L`. For `h ∈ L` put
`B_h(x, y) = ⟨x(h), y(h)⟩ − ⟨x(chc⁻¹), y(chc⁻¹)⟩`. If `T̄_s ≇ T̄_s^†∘c`, the `B_h` span all bilinear forms on `S × S′`
(`span_abstract`: polarisation, the eigenvalues `ϑ ≠ ϑ⁻¹` of `z`, Burnside's theorem, inner automorphisms of matrix
algebras); as `dim S = dim S′` (an order formula), some combination has zero left kernel. If `T̄_s ≅ T̄_s^†∘c`, then
`ν ≥ 1` by determinants, vanishing combinations are symmetric (`r` odd), and an alternating form on the sum of `S` and
the transported `S′` has left kernel of dimension `≤ 1`. Chebotarev makes these `h` second-half Frobenius classes.

Residual bound (`resBound`). Let `Σ_a θ_a B_{φ_a}` over the second-half Frobenius elements have left kernel of dimension
`≤ e ∈ {0, 1}`. A lift of `Φ` is restricted to a line `X_q = b_q Y` over a field `K` containing the residue field `k` of
`O₀`, with a formal parameter `σ` in `b`, and read over the dual numbers: a first-order kernel vector is a Selmer class
`ω` for the character `1 + εμ`, `μ = −Σ b_q d_q`. The tame relation `μ(γ)ω₀(φ) = μ(φ)ω₀(γ)` for `ω₀ = ω mod ε` at
second-half places, expanded in `σ`, shows that the leading part is unramified at `Q`, so lies in `S`, and reciprocity
(sum of local invariants, tame pairing) puts it in the left kernel. So at most `e` independent residual classes lift to
first order and `dim Sel_Q(T̄_s) ≤ 2rR + e`; linear algebra over `K[Y]` then shows that `det` (`e = 0`) or an adjugate
entry (`e = 1`) is not divisible by `Y^{4rR+1}`, and as `l^M > 4rR + 2` a lift has a unit coefficient in degree `≤ 4rR`.

This is the claim if `ν = 0`. If `ν ≥ 1` (always when `l = 2`), `thmC_of_parts` inducts over the lattices: all
first-half places are chosen at once, one unpaired per lattice, and `step` adds for each lattice in turn a second-half
place paired with that one. If no low coefficient of the determinant lies outside `𝔞 = λ l^ν O₀` (`λ` a uniformiser of
`O₀`), the presentation is evaluated in a finite local `O₀/𝔞`-algebra `A` with a nilpotent `τ`: the determinant
vanishes, an adjugate column is a Selmer class `x`, and a count gives a dual class `y` not killed by `l^ν τ^m`. Then
`B_h(x, y) ≠ 0` for some `h` in a small subgroup, Chebotarev gives a place with Frobenius in the class of `h`, and over
an extension of `A` by two nilpotent variables the new determinant is nonzero (`det_ne_zero`). `compare` keeps earlier
bounds.

Specialisation (`order_eval`). Given for `M ≥ M₀` finitely many polynomials with a coefficient of valuation `≤ μ` in
degree `≤ D`, and some of a second kind (automorphic side), there are `M`, `k ≤ M`, `e_q < k` with `l^{e_q}/φ(l^k) ≤ ε`
such that all values at `(ζ^{l^{e_q}} − 1)_q` are nonzero of normalised valuation at most the level plus `ε`, for every
primitive `l^k`-th root of unity `ζ` (substitute `X_q ↦ (1 + Y)^{l^{e_q}} − 1` with widely spaced `e_q`; then all but
boundedly many `k` work). The main theorem takes `ε` proportional to `ε′` and `ψ = ∏ ζ^{l^{e_q} d_q}`; `ζ^a` is again
primitive, and `#Q ≤ 4R`. Inputs (named hypotheses): Chebotarev, Poitou–Tate, cohomological dimension 2, tame quotients,
local duality, unramified orthogonality, finiteness, Euler characteristics, ray class characters, Bloch–Kato.

## Key steps

- **Choice of Frobenius classes** (`GlobalTheory.ChooseForms.chooseH`): elements `h_a ∈ L` whose classes, taken as
  second-half Frobenius classes, give a combination of the `B_h` with left kernel of dimension `0` (if `ν = 0`) or
  `≤ 1`. Irreducibility and the scalar `z` enter in `span_abstract` (`CocycleFormsSpanOddResidueChar.lean`, line 96).
- **Residual determinant bound** (`GlobalTheory.ResOrder.resBound`): a unit coefficient in degree `≤ 4rR` of a lift of
  the determinant, or of an adjugate entry. The argument is in `core` (same file, line 118): first-order deformation
  along a line with a formal parameter, tame relation, reciprocity.
- **One more place** (`GlobalTheory.NewPrime.step`): for `ν ≥ 1`, from a unit coefficient of an adjugate entry to a
  coefficient of the determinant outside `λ l^ν O₀`, after adding one second-half place. The enlarged determinant is
  shown nonzero in `det_ne_zero` (line 85 of the same file); the factor `l^ν` enters through `exists_bform_ne_zero`.
- **Persistence** (`GlobalTheory.Growth.compare`): when a second-half place is added, `u^{2r}·det Φ` lies in the ideal
  of `det Φ′` restricted to the old variables, modulo `λ l^ν`, for a polynomial `u` without constant term and with a
  unit coefficient on the paired variable; likewise for adjugate entries. The induction over the lattices needs this.
- **Specialisation** (`GlobalTheory.OrderEval.order_eval`): one level `M`, one order `l^k` and exponents `e_q` at which
  finitely many polynomials of the two kinds take nonzero values of valuation at most their level plus `ε`, for every
  primitive `l^k`-th root of unity. It turns coefficient bounds into one character and gives all prime-to-`l` powers.
