## Statement

There is a real constant `C` such that for every prime `ℓ`, every totally complex number field `L` containing a
primitive `ℓ`-th root of unity (a primitive fourth root of unity if `ℓ = 2`) and every integer `E ≥ 1`, the group
`H² = H²(G_{L,ℓ}, ℤ_ℓ(E+1))` is finite and `log #H² ≤ exp((2En)^C · log(2|d_L|))`, where `n = [L:ℚ]` and `d_L` is the
discriminant of `L`.

Here `G_{L,ℓ}` is the Galois group of the maximal extension of `L` unramified at every finite place not above `ℓ`,
`ℤ_ℓ(E+1)` is `ℤ_ℓ` with the `(E+1)`-st power of the cyclotomic character, and the cohomology is that of continuous
cochains. This group is usually written `H²(𝒪_L[1/ℓ], ℤ_ℓ(E+1))`, as étale cohomology, but the formal statement does
not include that identification. The roots of unity force `ℓ ≤ n + 1`.

The Lean theorem has four hypotheses, each a named proposition: a rank bound in the homology of the groups `GL_n(𝒪_K)`
(`BOdd`, below), Poitou–Tate duality for finite modules, and finiteness and Tate duality for the cohomology of finite
modules over finite extensions of `ℚ_ℓ`. The source has a theorem without hypotheses for three of them. For
Poitou–Tate duality it has one for a normalised form, and a theorem deducing the hypothesis from that form. Only the
existence of `C` is stated, and the constants chosen in the proofs give `C = 150`. We did not find a declaration that
states the bound without hypotheses.

## Context

Once identified with the étale cohomology of `𝒪_L[1/ℓ]`, an identification the source does not make, the group is the
target of the `ℓ`-adic Chern class on `K_{2E}(𝒪_L)`. No K-group occurs in the formal statement. The statement bounds
the order of the group in terms of the twist, the degree and the discriminant alone, with
`log log #H² ≤ (2En)^C · log(2|d_L|)`, at the primes `ℓ` with `μ_ℓ ⊂ L`, hence `ℓ ≤ [L:ℚ] + 1`. The proof bounds the
length of the twisted Tate–Shafarevich group `Ш¹(L, ℓ, E)` in the same way. Within the formal proof the proposition is
an input to later estimates, and about fifty files refer to it.

## Comparison with the literature

We compared the literature with an earlier description of the result: the bound for the étale group
`H²(𝒪_L[1/ℓ], ℤ_ℓ(E+1))`, together with a corestriction form for all fields and all primes. The statement here is
about Galois cohomology, it is an implication from four named hypotheses, and it has no corestriction form, so the
comparisons we made at all primes do not apply to it. The others apply to it through the identification with étale
cohomology. The difference of notation does not matter for them, but the source does not make the identification. With
that understood, the bound is not comparable with the published ones. Soulé (2003, Theorem 3), Bayer-Fluckiger, Emery
and Houriet (2015, Theorem 1.1) and Emery (2014, `E = 1`) set aside the small primes, which is where this statement
lives (`ℓ ≤ n + 1`, `ℓ = 2` included). The last two are bounds for K-groups, of which the étale `H²` is a quotient
after tensoring with `ℤ_ℓ`. On the other hand those bounds are uniform in `ℓ` and bound the product over `ℓ`, and the
statement here gives neither such a product bound nor any vanishing. They are explicit, except for Emery's constant.
The bound of Bayer-Fluckiger, Emery and Houriet has the same shape as the one here, and Emery's, for `E = 1`, is
smaller, though his gains are asymptotic, with the degree and the twist fixed. Soulé's bound is much larger in the
discriminant, except for imaginary quadratic fields and `ℚ`. The error in the prime cutoff of Soulé (2003) found by de
la Torre Durán is real, and `n(4E+1) + 1` is a safe cutoff. That these primes are omitted holds for the general
estimates cited here, and we do not claim it for every published bound. Finiteness of the group is classical.

We found no published statement that implies the bound. An independent second check, which agrees with this
comparison, found none either, and does not take this to mean that the bound, and still less its method, is not in
print. Our search of later work was partial: we read the lists of works citing Soulé (1999) and Bayer-Fluckiger, Emery
and Houriet, but could not retrieve the list for Soulé (2003), and we have not opened J. Houriet's 2010 thesis. Our
own impression, which rests on no source, is that keeping the small primes in Soulé's method looks within reach. We
would like to know whether any published bound, or Houriet's thesis, covers these primes.

## Sketch of the proof

Write `q = ℓ^ν` and `κ = 1 + ℓ^c`, and write `r₂(K)` and `h_K` for the number of complex places and the class number
of `K`. The theorem applies `lemmaK_of_steps` to twenty-one propositions stated in `LemmaKOfSteps.lean`, each proved
separately.

**From H² to Ш¹** (`sFinal`). Let `Ш¹(L, ℓ, E)` be the group of classes in `H¹(G_{L,ℓ}, ℚ_ℓ/ℤ_ℓ(−E))` that vanish on a
decomposition group at each place above `ℓ`. The three duality hypotheses, used with finite coefficients and passed to
the limit, give `length H² ≤ length Ш¹ + Σ_{v|ℓ} length H⁰(L_v, ℚ_ℓ/ℤ_ℓ(−E))`. There are at most `n` places `v`, and
each local term is at most `2 + log_ℓ n + E`. As `ℓ ≤ n + 1`, it is enough to bound `length Ш¹(L, ℓ, E) · log ℓ` in
the same shape. The duality hypotheses are used nowhere else.

**A larger field** (`SLayer`). Replace `L` by the layer `L′` of degree `ℓ^m` of its cyclotomic `ℤ_ℓ`-extension, with
`4E + 4 ≤ ℓ^m ≤ ℓ(4E + 4)`. If `L′` has `ℓ^c` roots of unity of `ℓ`-power order, then `2E + 1 < ℓ^c ≤ 2[L′:ℚ]`,
`r₂(L′) > 2E + 1`, `|d_{L′}| ≤ |d_L|^{[L′:L]} · (ℓ^c)^{[L′:ℚ]}`, and `length Ш¹(L) ≤ length Ш¹(L′)` by a descent
formula: the two lengths are those of the cokernels of `g − 1` and `g^{ℓ^m} − 1` for one operator `g`.

**Classes as eigen-homomorphisms** (`sSha`). Restriction to `L′(μ_{ℓ^∞})` is injective on `Ш¹(L′, ℓ, E)`, and a class
in the image lives on a layer `F = L′(μ_q)`. There it is a function `φ` from the primes of `F` to `ℤ/q` that vanishes
on principal divisors and satisfies `φ(γ⁻¹P) = κ^E φ(P)`, where `γ` generates `Gal(F/L′)` and acts on `μ_q` by
`ζ ↦ ζ^κ`. So a bound for the number of such `φ`, uniform in `ν`, bounds `#Ш¹(L′, ℓ, E)`.

**Counting through twisted norms** (`sCount`). Let `W` be the group of `α ∈ F^×` with divisor in `(γ⁻¹ − κ^E)Div(F)`,
modulo `(γ⁻¹ − κ^E)F^×` and roots of unity. Its order is that of `Cl(F)/(γ⁻¹ − κ^E)`, whose `ℓ`-part bounds the number
of `φ`, times that of the same quotient of the units. Because `L′` is totally complex, the latter is
`(κ^{E[F:L′]} − 1)^{r₂(L′)}/(κ^E − 1)`. With the twisted norm `N(α) = ∏_j γ^j(α)^{κ^{Ej}}` this gives
`#{φ} · q^{r₂(L′)} ≤ B · ℓ^X`, where `B` is the number of classes of the `N(α)^{ℓ^b}` modulo `q`-th powers, and
`X = (b + v_ℓ(E))·(2[L′:ℚ] + log₂ h_{L′} + 2) + c + v_ℓ(E)` does not depend on `ν`.

**Twisted norms and the homology of GL** (`sFewOf`). For `b = 2E + (2E+1)(c + 2E + 1)`,
`B ≤ q^{r₂(L′)} · t_{2E+1} · t_{2E}`, with `t_m` the order of the torsion of `H_m(GL_{4E+6}(𝒪_{L′}); ℤ)`. Several
statements go into this. `STheta` gives, over a finite field `k ⊃ μ_q` and for `v : k^× → ℤ/q`, an additive class:
functionals `θ_V` on the `(2E+1)`-chains modulo `q` of the bar complex of `GL(V)`, for all `V` of finite dimension,
which vanish on boundaries, are invariant under isomorphisms, are additive on direct sums, and take the value
`x^E · v(det g)` on the prism over `g` of a fixed `2E`-cycle of `ℤ/q` acting by the scalars `ζ^x`. `SClass` gives, for
each `α`, a cycle `y_α` of degree `2E + 1` of some `GL_N(𝒪_{L′})` modulo `q`, on whose reduction at each prime `P` of
`F` of degree one over `L′`, outside a finite set, `θ` has the value `pe · v(N(α) mod P)`. Here
`pe = ∏_{j ≤ 2E, j ≠ E} (κ^E − κ^j)` divides a fixed power of `ℓ`. `SPhi` says that `θ` composed with reduction is a
primitive family `Φ = (Φ_n)` on the chains of the groups `GL_n(𝒪_{L′})`: it vanishes on boundaries, is compatible with
stabilization, and satisfies `Φ_{dn} ∘ ψ^d = d · Φ_n` on cycles, where `ψ^d(g) = 1_d ⊗ g`. Take `v` with kernel the
`q`-th powers. If `ℓ^{2E} Φ` agrees on `y_α` and `y_{α′}` at every `P`, then `N(α)^{ℓ^b}/N(α′)^{ℓ^b}` is a `q`-th
power modulo every `P`, hence in `F` (`SDetect`, by the density of Frobenius elements at primes of degree one). So `B`
is at most the number of values on cycles of `ℓ^{2E} Φ`, for one primitive family with values in a power of `ℤ/q`.

**Counting values** (`sUpperOf`). A Bockstein argument passes to integral classes at the cost of the factor `t_{2E}`.
On `H_{2E+1}(GL_n(𝒪_{L′}); ℤ)`, `ℓ^{2E} Φ(x) = u · Φ(z(x))` with `z(x) = ∏_{t<2E} (ψ^ℓ − ℓ^{t+2})(x)`, and by `BOdd`
the `z(x)` span a group of rank at most `r₂(L′)`. A group of rank `r` with torsion of order `t` has at most `q^r · t`
images in a group killed by `q`, and homological stability gives the same torsion for all `n ≥ 4E + 6`. The source
proves `BOdd` as `bOdd`. Judging from the statements of its steps, since we did not read the proofs, it goes through
invariant differential forms sorted by weight under `ψ^d`.

**Torsion and assembly** (`sTors`, `sNum`). For totally complex `K`, `N ≤ 4E + 6` and `m ≤ 2E + 1`,
`log #H_m(GL_N(𝒪_K); ℤ)_tors ≤ exp((2E[K:ℚ])^{24} log(2|d_K|))`, every prime included. Here the group acts on the
acyclic nerve of the cover by perfect Hermitian forms, with counted orbits and stabilizers of order at most
`3^{[K:ℚ]N²}`. In `sNum` the factors `q^{r₂(L′)}` cancel, which gives `ℓ^{length Ш¹(L′)} ≤ t_{2E+1} · t_{2E} · ℓ^X`.
The bounds of the second step and `log h_{L′} = O([L′:ℚ] log(2|d_{L′}|))` then give the bound for `L`. The density of
Frobenius elements, homological stability and the class number bound are theorems of the source.

## Key steps

- **From H² to Ш¹** (`UpperTheory.V.ZK.sFinal`): `length H²` is at most `length Ш¹(L, ℓ, E)` plus local terms
  polynomial in `n` and `E`. The three duality hypotheses enter here and only here, through
  `UpperTheory.V.LDataCardLeFinrank.VLen_le_shaLen_add`.
- **Classes as eigen-homomorphisms** (`PositivityTheory.QuaternionShaBounds.sSha`): a bound, uniform in `ν`, for the
  functions `φ` on the primes of `L′(μ_{ℓ^ν})` with `φ(γ⁻¹P) = κ^E φ(P)` that vanish on principal divisors bounds
  `#Ш¹(L′, ℓ, E)`. The cohomological group becomes a count on class groups.
- **Count through twisted norms** (`UpperTheory.V.ZK.sCount`): `#{φ} · q^{r₂(L′)} ≤ B · ℓ^X` with `X` independent of
  `ν`. The factor `q^{r₂(L′)}` comes from the units of the layer over a totally complex base, and the argument works
  because the homological count has the same factor.
- **A cycle with prescribed residues** (`UpperTheory.V.ZK.sClassOf`): for `α` with divisor in `(γ⁻¹ − κ^E)Div(F)`, a
  cycle of `GL_N(𝒪_{L′})` modulo `q` on whose reductions the additive classes take the value `pe · v(N(α) mod P)`. It
  is glued from prisms over a relation among `2E + 2` embeddings of `ℤ/q` in `(ℤ/q)²`.
- **Separation by residues** (`LateralTheory.Q.SFewOfCut.sFewOf`): distinct classes of twisted norms give distinct
  values of one primitive family, hence `B ≤ q^{r₂(L′)} · t_{2E+1} · t_{2E}`. It uses the density of Frobenius
  elements at primes of degree one (`LateralTheory.Q.SDetectCut.goal`).
- **The rank bound** (`UpperTheory.V.ZK.bOdd`): for totally complex `K` and even `k` with `k + 1 < r₂(K)`, the classes
  `∏_{t<k} (ψ^ℓ − ℓ^{t+2})(x)`, `x ∈ H_{k+1}(GL_n(𝒪_K); ℤ)`, `n ≥ 2k + 6`, span a group of rank at most `r₂(K)`. It
  gives the exponent `r₂` in the count of values (`UpperTheory.V.ZK.sUpperOf`).

## Caveats

`UpperTheory.V.ZK.lemmaK` is a proved theorem. For its four hypotheses the source has the theorems
`UpperTheory.V.ZK.bOdd`, `PositivityTheory.Lit1.PTN.poitouTateNormalised_holds` (a normalised form, from which
`GlobalTheory.PoitouTateConj.poitouTateDuality_of_normalised` deduces the hypothesis), and `top_locfin` and `top_tate`
in `CompanionTheory.LocalTateOrthogonality.Gcoh`. We found no declaration stating the bound without hypotheses:
`UpperTheory.V.ZK.lemmaK_litN_alt` still assumes a bundle of published theorems, which another theorem supplies. We
read in full the statements, `lemmaK_of_steps`, `sFinal`, `sNum`, `sCount`, `sFewOf`, `sUpperOf`, `torsAll`, the last
step of `sSha`, and the proofs of `SLayer` and `SDetect`. Everything below these we read as statements only, in
particular the cycle, the additive classes, `bOdd`, the torsion bound and the duality theorems. A text search of some
9,400 Lean files found no `sorry` and no `axiom` declaration. We compiled nothing, and the value `C = 150` was read
off three proofs.
