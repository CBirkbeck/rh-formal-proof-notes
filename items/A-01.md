## Statement

There are natural numbers `C, A` such that for every even `e ≥ 2`, `Σ_ℓ log ℓ · V_ℓ(1, e) ≤ C·(e + 1)^A`, the sum over
any finite set of primes `ℓ ≥ 5` with `(ℓ − 1) ∤ 2e`. Here `V_ℓ(1, e)` is the normalized length of a Selmer group of
`ℚ_ℓ/ℤ_ℓ(−e)` over `ℚ`; a comparison with K-theory that is not in the source identifies it with `v_ℓ(#K_{2e}(ℤ))`.

Precisely (`bound`): for a prime `ℓ`, let `O` be the ring of integers of a finite extension of `ℚ_ℓ`, of ramification
index `e_O`, and `ψ` a Dirichlet character mod `N` with values in `O`. `Ш(ψ, e)` is the set of classes in
`H¹(Gal(ℚ̄/ℚ), (O ⊗ ℚ_ℓ/ℤ_ℓ)(ψ⁻¹χ_ℓ^{−e}))` that vanish on all inertia groups and on the decomposition groups above
the primes dividing `ℓN`, and `V_ℓ(ψ, e) = length_O Ш(ψ, e) / e_O`. The bound is for `ψ = 1`, `N = 2` and any rings
`O`; the lengths are finite. If `ℓ ≥ 5`, `e ≥ 2` is even and `(ℓ − 1) | 2e`, then the group defined in the same way
with no condition above `2` is zero (`propZ`); it contains `Ш(1, e)`, which is therefore zero.

Character form (`corGK`): `Σ_{ℓ∈L} log ℓ · Σ_{ker ψ = H} V_ℓ(ψ, e) ≤ 2c·φ([(ℤ/N)^× : H])·(N(e + 1))^A·log(pp′)`, with
`c, A` depending on `K`, for all `e`, `N`, `H ⊂ (ℤ/N)^×`, primes `p ≠ p′` prime to `30·N·d_K` and finite sets `L` of
primes with some `ψ` of kernel `H` admissible (`GD`): `K` imaginary quadratic, `ℓ ∤ 2·d_K·h_K`, `N` even, prime to
`d_K`, `ℓ ∤ ord ψ`, `ψ_ℓ² = 1` (`ψ_ℓ`: `ℓ`-component), `ψ(−1) = (−1)^e`, `(ℓ − 1) ∤ 2e`, `K ⊄ ℚ(μ_ℓ)·ℚ(ψ/ψ_ℓ)`.

## Why it might matter

For odd `ℓ`, Poitou–Tate duality and the comparison of K-theory with étale cohomology (Weibel 2005, Theorem 70) relate
the length of `Ш(1, e)` over `ℤ_ℓ` to `v_ℓ(#K_{2e}(ℤ))`; their equality uses in addition that over `ℚ` the classes
that are trivial above `ℓ` account for the whole group, a step the first reading did not check against a published
source. That identification is not in the formal proof; granting it, the two statements over `ℚ` bound
`log #K_{4k}(ℤ)`, away from `2` and `3`, by a polynomial in `k`. These groups are tied to the even eigenspaces of
class groups of `ℚ(μ_ℓ)` that Vandiver's conjecture predicts to vanish. Under the same identification the character
form concerns parts of wild kernels of abelian fields.

## What the checks found

The first reading compared a differently phrased statement: a bound for the order of `K_{2e}(ℤ)` away from `2`, `3`,
`7` and the primes with `(ℓ − 1) | 2e`. The Lean bounds Selmer classes (`ℓ = 7` included); reading them as
`v_ℓ(#K_{2e}(ℤ))` needs Poitou–Tate duality applied to these groups and the comparison of K-theory with étale
cohomology; the source does not make this identification (it has Poitou–Tate statements for a differently defined
group, and no K-group occurs in the statement), so the comparison holds for this page only through that
identification. Its verdict: improves. The published bounds it names are bounds for K-groups and for étale cohomology
groups: for `log #K_{2e}(ℤ)` they are exponential in a power of `e` (Soulé 1999, Theorem 3; Soulé 2003, Proposition
4(iv); de la Torre Durán, a 2026 preprint) and set aside `5 ≤ ℓ ≤ 4e + 2`. Soulé's constants are explicit; those of
the preprint and those here are not; `K_4(ℤ) = K_8(ℤ) = 0` and Vandiver's conjecture, verified below `2^31`, are
stronger where they apply; the vanishing case is known in substance (Weibel 2005, Theorem 105). Confidence high over
`ℚ`, medium for the character form. The second opinion: improves, medium confidence (polynomial against exponential
growth; absence of prior art less certain); Vandiver's conjecture does not follow. It adds one precision (the
improvement on Soulé's bounds is asymptotic; his explicit constants are not recovered), agrees that the K-theory
identification is external, and notes that the condition above `2` is automatic and that excluding `7` is unnecessary.
The two agree on the verdict, not on the confidence. Open: can `C` and `A` be computed from the proof? Does the
Iwasawa-theoretic literature bound the even-character parts for abelian fields?

## How the formal proof goes

*From characters to ℚ, and two primes.* `bound` is `bound_of` applied to `corGK`: the character form with `N = 2`
(only the trivial character), `K = ℚ(√−3)`, `p = 7`, `p′ = 11`; each `ℓ` in the sum is admissible because `d_K = −3`,
`h_K = 1` and `3` ramifies in `K` but not in `ℚ(μ_ℓ)`. `large_of` deduces from `Bound` (an implication; its conclusion
is not declared as a separate theorem): if `e_O = 1` and `log ℓ > C(e + 1)^A`, then `Ш(1, e) = 0`. The character form
follows (`U3.corGK`) from `PropGK`: for each prime `p ∤ 30·N·d_K` there is an integer `NN_p ≠ 0` with
`log|NN_p| ≤ c·φ([(ℤ/N)^× : H])·(N(e + 1))^A·log p` and `Σ_ψ V_ℓ(ψ, e) ≤ 2·v_ℓ(NN_p)` for every admissible `ℓ ≠ p`.
Each `ℓ` differs from `p` or from `p′`, and `Σ_ℓ log ℓ · v_ℓ(n) ≤ log|n|`.

*The integer.* `propGK_of_numberU3U_of_oneCharU` takes a place `w` of `K` above `p`, `q = Nw`, a Frobenius `σ` at `w`,
and for each `ψ` the Eisenstein trace `t_ψ = Ψ(σ) + 1 + Ψ(σ)⁻¹`, where `Ψ = χ_ℓ^e ψ`, so `Ψ(σ) = q^e ψ(q)`. Call
`ρ : G_K → GL₃(ℚ̄_ℓ)` *admitted* if it is irreducible and `ρ ⊗ χ_ℓ⁻¹` is attached to a conjugate self-dual cuspidal
automorphic representation of `GL₃(𝔸_K)`, of level `N`, with exponents `(e, 0, −e)`, unlinked at `ℓ`; or if
`ρ ≅ W|_{G_K} ⊕ 1` for an irreducible two-dimensional `W` of `G_ℚ` of level `N·|d_K|` with `W ⊗ χ_ℓ^{−e}` attached to
a newform of weight `2e + 1` (with a condition on the determinant of `W` and a clause on its restrictions to imaginary
quadratic fields). (1) `OneCharU_s`: if `(ψ, e, ℓ, K)` is admissible and `tr ρ(σ) ≠ t_ψ` for all admitted `ρ`, then
`V_ℓ(ψ, e) ≤ 2·Σ_{ρ∈X} v_ℓ(tr ρ(σ) − t_ψ)` for a finite set `X` of pairwise non-isomorphic admitted `ρ`. (2)
`NumberU3U_s`: an integer `NN ≠ 0` of the size above with `|NN|_ℓ` at most `∏_ψ ∏_{ρ∈X_ψ} |tr ρ(σ) − t_ψ|_ℓ` for all
such sets and all `ℓ ∤ 2p`. `numberU3U_of_prints` gives every admitted `ρ`, injectively on isomorphism classes, an
index in a finite `Aut(ℂ)`-stable family `(s_i)`, `i ∈ S`, with `tr ρ(σ) = s_i`, `|s_i| ≤ √q + 2`, `q^e·s_i` integral
and `#S ≤ c(N(e + 1))^A`; then `NN = ∏_{ψ,i} q^e(s_i − t_ψ)` is an integer, nonzero as
`|t_ψ| ≥ q^e − 1 − q^{−e} > √q + 2` for `q ≥ 7`. The count of `S` over `K` reduces (`card_k_of_printed_results`) to
cohomology of congruence subgroups (theorems of Clozel and Borel, a bound at level `3M`); over `ℚ` it passes through
auxiliary imaginary quadratic fields (`countQ_alt`). `√q + 2` is from the Jacquet–Shalika bound for the unitary twist.

*One character* (`oneCharU_of_katoTwoW_rig_admI_automorphy`). After reduction to `O` unramified (using `ℓ ∤ ord ψ`),
the proof studies lifts of `Ψ̄ ⊕ 1 ⊕ Ψ̄⁻¹` that are conjugate self-dual over `K`, trivial on the inertia elements away
from `ℓ` that act trivially residually, subject at `ℓ` to an ideal of the local lifting ring, and normalized at two
marked elements. The universal ring `S.R` exists (`exists_ring`), and `exists_model` gives a `ℤ`-graded `O`-algebra
`A → S.R`, graded by conjugation with `diag(t, 1, t⁻¹)`, with the diagonal lift as point `x`, a formal chart at `x`,
and homogeneous equations: one more than coordinates in weight `−1`, one fewer in weights `1` and `2`.

- (a) `vlen_le`: the `b`-torsion of `Ш(ψ, e)` embeds in normalized cocycles on the lower triangular entries of weight
  `−1`; each gives a point derivation at `x` modulo `b` that lifts to `O`, so `length Ш(ψ, e) ≤ cotLen₋₁`, the length
  of the weight `−1` cotangent lattice at `x` modulo the weight `−1` equations (`LemmaM'`).
- (b) `LemmaM` (`main_holds`): under conditions (M1)–(M5), `cotLen₋₁ ≤ 2·Σ_ξ v(ξ(a))`, summed over the torus orbits of
  *two-signed* points (killing neither all negative nor all positive weights), for `a` of weight `0` with `x(a) = 0`
  and `ξ(a) ≠ 0` at these points; `model_upper` takes `a = tr r(σ) − tr r₀(σ)`. The proof (`core_holds`) squeezes an
  Euler characteristic on a multigraded ring between a count at `x` and local terms.
- (c) `twoPt_shape`: the two-signed points are the lifts neither upper nor lower triangular; each is irreducible on
  `G_K` or of the form `W ⊕ μ`, and two with isomorphic restrictions to `G_K` lie in one torus orbit.

The conditions are arithmetic. (M1) combines the counts of the model with the vanishing of tangent vectors of weight
`≤ 0` at `x`. Finiteness of the weight-zero ring (M2) and automorphy of both kinds of two-signed points form
`AutomorphyUnramifiedCoefficientsAlt`: residually reducible automorphy lifting for this problem, with no Steinberg
place, obtained through `ends0_alt`. (M3) uses that a lift both upper and lower triangular is the diagonal one (a use
of `ℓ ∤ h_K`). (M4), proportionality of derivations at two-signed points, follows from the vanishing of adjoint
Bloch–Kato Selmer groups (`RigidityFirstCase`; for `W ⊕ μ`, `RigiditySecondCase` and Kato's theorem).

*The case where ℓ − 1 divides 2e* (`propZ`). It suffices to kill classes of order `ℓ`. If `(ℓ − 1) | e`, such a class
is a homomorphism unramified outside `ℓ`; it dies on the kernel of `χ_ℓ` (Kronecker–Weber), and restriction to that
kernel is injective. Otherwise `ℓ ≡ 1 mod 4`, the class restricted to `ℚ(√ℓ)` factors through the class group, and
`h(ℚ(√ℓ)) < ℓ` (`hQuad`, from `h ≤ √d·(1 + log √d)`).

## Key steps

- **Graded cotangent bound** (`UpperTheory.V.Alg.M.main_holds`): `MainCotSpan.lean`, line 32. With one more equation
  than coordinates in weight `−1`, the weight `−1` cotangent length at the fixed point is at most twice the sum of
  valuations of a weight-zero element at the two-signed points. Selmer lengths become trace differences.
- **Cotangent bound on the deformation model** (`UpperTheory.V.U3.model_upper`): `UnitaryThreeGroups/ModelUpper.lean`,
  line 103. Derives the five conditions for the graded model of the lifts of `Ψ̄ ⊕ 1 ⊕ Ψ̄⁻¹` and applies the bound to
  the trace at `σ`; with `vlen_le` (`VlenLe.lean`, line 52) the Selmer length is bounded by trace differences.
- **Bound for one character** (`UpperTheory.V.Z.J.oneCharU_of_katoTwoW_rig_admI_automorphy`):
  `DenseThresholdCases/FOneCharU.lean`, line 75. Proves `V_ℓ(ψ, e) ≤ 2·Σ v_ℓ(tr ρ(σ) − t_ψ)` from nine named inputs
  (cohomology, three algebra lemmas, Kato's theorem, two rigidity statements, admissibility, automorphy).
- **Automorphy on the Eisenstein component** (`UpperTheory.V.Aut.AutomorphyUnramifiedCoefficientsAlt`):
  `DenseThresholdCases/AutomorphyUnramifiedCoefficients.lean`, line 23 (a proposition; derived at `CohInp.lean`, line
  18, from two named propositions, which the proof of `corGK` supplies; no declaration states it without hypotheses).
  The weight-zero ring is finite and the two-signed points are automorphic.
- **The congruence integer** (`UpperTheory.GalN.V.Win.frobenius_trace_norm_nonzero_log_bound`):
  `AdmissibleResidues/FrobeniusTraceCountBoundOfRatEigensystemCount.lean`, line 329. From the family `(s_i)`:
  `NN ≠ 0`, `log|NN| ≤ #orbit·#S·(2e·log q + 1)`, and the `ℓ`-adic inequality. As `#S` is polynomial, so is `log|NN|`.
- **Specialisation to the rationals** (`UpperTheory.V.SmallClassNumbers.bound`): `SmallClassNumbers/CorGKOf.lean`,
  line 27, by `bound_of` (`ClassNumberThree.lean`, line 88). Takes `N = 2`, `K = ℚ(√−3)`, `p = 7`, `p′ = 11` and
  checks admissibility for every `ℓ ≥ 5` with `(ℓ − 1) ∤ 2e`. It fixes the primes covered and the condition above `2`.
