## Statement

Let `R` be a complete discrete valuation ring of characteristic zero, or a complete noetherian normal local domain of
characteristic zero with a prime `I` such that `R` is `I`-adically complete and `R/I` a principal ideal domain; let
`G/R` be semi-abelian with a cubical symmetric `L`, abelian generic fibre on which `L` is ample with `K(L)` trivial, and
`G mod I` an extension of an abelian scheme by a split torus. In the first case, let the cubical structure of `L` have
an even trivialisation on the torus of the closed fibre. Outside the first case, let some section of `L` vanish
identically on no fibre, and let `G` have endomorphisms `w`, `w′` with `w + w′ = 1`, `L ≅ (1, w)^*Λ` and
`(w × 1)^*Λ ≅ (1 × w′)^*Λ`, where `Λ = m^*L ⊗ p_1^*L^{−1} ⊗ p_2^*L^{−1} ⊗ 0^*L` on `G × G`. Then `(G, L)` has a
degeneration datum (`B`, `M`, a lattice `X`, `c : X → B(R)`, periods `ψ`), a uniformisation by it, and, for the generic
fibre `A`, every `k ≥ 1` and points in an algebraically closed field `F ⊇ Frac R`, `A[k] ≅ Q_k` with the Weil pairing,
where `Q_k` is the group of classes of pairs `(y, g)`, `y ∈ X`, `g` an `F`-point of the Raynaud extension with
`g^k = ι(y)`, modulo `ι(X)`: a special case of Faltings–Chai, Chapter II, with the torsion description of their III.7.3.
Converse: on the source's proper models of unitary Shimura varieties, for `O′` of characteristic zero, a field or a
complete discrete valuation ring, and arc data over `O′` satisfying the source's conditions (an action of the ring of
integers of the CM field, `ψ₀` even with invertible values, a positive definite hermitian form `b` with a divisibility
condition, torsion labels, compatibility with signature and level), the monomial periods `ψ₀(y)·X^{b(y,y)/2}` over
`O′[[X]]` give a unique arc with these data. Existence: over every complete noetherian normal local domain of
characteristic zero every degeneration datum comes from a semi-abelian scheme, a case of Mumford's theorem (principal
polarisation, split torus with constant character group).

## Why it might matter

The formal proof produces degenerating abelian varieties with prescribed periods without Mumford's relatively complete
model and quotient, and without a rigid-analytic quotient. According to text references (not a dependency check), these
families feed the boundary charts of the Siegel toroidal compactification, which the source proves rather than assumes.

## What the checks found

The first reading keeps two verdicts. Forward direction: standard proof, specialised (Faltings–Chai, Chapter II, in the
principal, split, characteristic-zero case), where `ψ` is read off the expansion of the one theta function; the torsion
description is organised differently, without the orthogonality theorem or the dual abelian variety. Confidence medium
(Lean read at skeleton level; Faltings–Chai known only through later accounts and recollection). Converse and existence:
they differ from the published proofs compared: the abelian variety is found as a point of a moduli space of theta
points, from theta series, by an identity principle through `ℂ` and a closure criterion, its data recognised afterwards
on a proper model; no relatively complete model and no quotient. Confidence high that this is not the argument of
Mumford, Faltings–Chai or Raynaud–Bosch–Lütkebohmert as described in the sources read; low to medium that no proof by
theta series is published. The second opinion cannot tell (medium confidence): the forward method is known, and the
converse is known in spirit through Mumford and Chai, whose 1985 Appendix III it calls a substantial precedent for the
closure criterion, the exact coset criterion unverified. They agree on the forward direction and disagree on the
converse; neither establishes novelty. Open: do Chai's appendices or Mumford's *Equations* III contain this construction?

## How the formal proof goes

Objects. A degeneration datum over `(R, I)` is an abelian scheme `B` with a symmetric rigidified ample `M`, `K(M)`
trivial, a free abelian group `X` of finite rank, `c : X → B(R)`, and trivialisations `ψ(χ)` of `c(χ)^*M^{−1}` over
`Frac R`, with `ψ` even, `τ(a, b) = ψ(a + b)ψ(a)^{−1}ψ(b)^{−1}` bimultiplicative, `ψ(χ) ∈ I` for `χ ≠ 0`, `ψ(χ) ∈ I^n` for
almost all `χ`. A uniformisation presents each `G mod I^{e+1}` as an extension of `B` by the split torus (the Raynaud
extension) and matches sections of `L^k` with families `(σ_α)` with `σ_{α+kμ} = ψ(μ)^k τ(μ, α) T^*_{c(μ)} σ_α`.

Forward, discrete valuation ring. (1) Lift the torus and the abelian quotient of the closed fibre to every thickening,
the cubical bundle descending to the quotient. (2) Algebraise the quotients to `B` with ample `M`, `K(M)` trivial; this
gives the Raynaud extension and `c`. (3) Expand the theta section `θ` of `L`, unique up to scalars, along the torus:
`θ = Σ_α f_α`, every `f_α ≠ 0`. (4) Each twisted bundle on `B` has a one-dimensional space of sections, so `f_y` is a
multiple of the translate of `f_0` by `c(y)`: the multiplier is `ψ(y)`. Evenness, bimultiplicativity of `τ`, finiteness
and positivity follow from the symmetry of `θ`, the cubical structure and convergence. (5) All degrees `k` follow, over
the algebraic closure, from weight spaces and irreducibility of the theta group. (6) A class in `Q_k` acts on families
of Fourier coefficients; a unique `k`-torsion point induces that operator; a count against `k^{2g}` gives a bijection;
the Weil pairing is the commutator pairing. General base: choose a complete discrete valuation ring `V ⊃ R` with
`m_V ∩ R = I`, build the tower over `R`, compare with `V`, and descend `ψ` using the section non-vanishing on fibres.

Converse along an arc. (7) From the arc data, write formal theta series (theta constants and theta coordinates of
torsion points), integral and algebraic over `O′[[X]]`. (8) A polynomial vanishing on the theta points of complex
abelian varieties with this structure vanishes on the series: descend to a countable ring, embed it in `ℂ`, specialise
the parameter to small values where the series converge to classical theta values. (9) Over an algebraically closed
field of characteristic zero, a point of the closure of the theta points with a non-zero coordinate in every coset of a
suitable subgroup of the index lattice is a theta point; properness is forced through a semi-abelian model and the
forward theory. (10) This gives a point of the moduli scheme over the fraction field of a finite normal extension of
`O′[[X]]`. (11) Its data on every complete discrete valuation ring are the prescribed ones; properness and normality of
the model, with extension of homomorphisms, give the arc and its uniqueness.

Existence. Embed the datum as an idempotent factor of one of unitary type (tensoring with a positive definite unimodular
lattice, `E₈`); after a finite extension, the proper models and the arcs give a proper modification of `Spec R` with a
semi-abelian scheme having the right data on every trait; descend it to `R` and cut out the factor. No relatively
complete model and no quotient, formal or rigid, occurs. The argument uses semistable reduction, proper models from
Zarhin's trick, complex uniformisation and the forward theory.

## Key steps

- **Periods from one theta function** (`GlobalTheory.UU.exists_degData_fe_one`, the resulting degeneration datum with
  the functional equation in degree one): `ψ(y)` is the multiplier comparing the Fourier coefficient `f_y` with the
  translate of `f_0`, using that each twisted bundle has a one-dimensional space of sections; this is where the
  principal case simplifies the published argument.
- **Torsion and Weil pairing** (`GlobalTheory.TorsionIso.torsion_eq`): `A[k] ≅ Q_k` through operators on Fourier
  families and a count, the pairing as a commutator pairing; no orthogonality theorem and no dual abelian variety.
- **General base** (`GlobalTheory.PrimeUnif.exists_unif`): reduction to one discrete valuation ring and descent of `ψ`,
  which needs two extra hypotheses: a section of `L` vanishing on no fibre, and the endomorphisms `w`, `w′` of the
  Statement, from which the even trivialisation on the closed fibre is obtained.
- **Identity principle through ℂ** (`GlobalTheory.ArcIdentity.aeval_arcTx_eq_zero_of_vanishC`): algebraic relations among
  theta values of complex abelian varieties transfer to the formal theta series by specialising the parameter.
- **Closure criterion** (`GlobalTheory.Ex.ClosurePoint.exists_full_of_closure_of_cosets`): a point of the closure of the
  theta points with a non-zero coordinate in every coset is the theta point of an abelian variety. The second opinion
  points to Chai's 1985 appendices as the place to compare.
- **Existence from data** (`GlobalTheory.Mum.has_of_base`): every degeneration datum over a complete normal local domain
  of characteristic zero comes from a semi-abelian scheme, through a proper modification and descent.

## What was checked, and what was not

The first reading read the Lean statements and the top two or three levels of each proof, with the statements of the
lemmas named, not their bodies (tower and algebraisation, complex specialisation, closure criterion, descent from the
modification), nor how the complex abelian variety with degenerate periods is obtained in step 8. A text search found no
`sorry` or `axiom` in the folders concerned; nothing was compiled. Seven of the declarations linked or named here are
implications from named statements: the forward ones from bundles of facts on abelian schemes, the general base also
from algebraisation statements and the discrete valuation ring case, and the three of the converse from seven statements
(two bundles of facts, the discrete valuation ring case with its torsion description, extension of homomorphisms of
semi-abelian schemes, two statements on theta points, complex uniformisation). The source has declarations without
hypotheses for all of these (`GlobalTheory.T.all_six_abelian_scheme_laws_hold`, `GlobalTheory.LN.ega_holds`,
`GlobalTheory.uniformisation_dvr_unconditional`, `GlobalTheory.arcFam_closed`). That its models satisfy the converse
along arcs is `GlobalTheory.partI_closed`, which rests on semistable reduction over `p`-adic fields, proved in the
source; that proof was not read. A general statement of Mumford's construction in the source is used nowhere; what is
proved is narrower. Faltings–Chai, Mumford (1972), Raynaud (1970), Bosch–Lütkebohmert, the book of Lan cited by Madapusi
Pera, and SGA 7 IX were not read at first hand; web pages were read in summary form.
