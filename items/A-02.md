## Statement

Let `χ` be a primitive Dirichlet character of conductor `f` and order `o`, let `n ≥ 2`, and let `q ≡ 3 mod 8` and
`q′ ≡ 7 mod 16` be primes not dividing `f`. Put `N = lcm(2^{n+2}, f)·q·q′` and
`E = ℚ(μ_{2^{n+2}})⁺·ℚ(χ)·ℚ(√−q)·ℚ(√−q′)`, where `ℚ(χ)` is the fixed field of the kernel of `χ`. Then `E` is a CM
field of conductor `N`.

For a prime `ℓ` and `e ≥ 1`, `Ш¹(E, ℓ, e)` is the group of classes in `H¹(G_S, ℚ_ℓ/ℤ_ℓ(−e))` vanishing on the
decomposition groups above `ℓ`, where `G_S` is the Galois group of the maximal extension of `E` unramified at the
finite places not above `ℓ`. Lengths are over `ℤ_ℓ`. For odd `ℓ`, `Ш⁺` and `Ш⁻` are the `(−1)^e`- and
`(−1)^{e+1}`-eigenspaces of complex conjugation. Let `p` be the least prime `≥ 7` with `p ≡ 3 mod 4` and `p ∤ f`, let
`h` be the class number of `ℚ(√−p)`, and let `p₁` be the least prime `≡ 1` modulo the odd part of `o` with `p₁ ∤ 2fp`.
Then `B(χ)` is `{3}` together with the odd primes dividing `o·f·p·h·p₁`.

The main case is the plus part (`SumOut`). There are `C` and primes `r₁, r₂`, depending on `χ` only, such that for all
`n, t ≥ 2` and all `q, q′` greater than each of `2t+1, f, o, p, h, p₁, r₁, r₂`, the groups below have finite length
and `Σ_{ℓ odd, ℓ ∉ B(χ)} log ℓ · Σ_{1≤e<t} length Ш⁺(E, ℓ, e) ≤ (N·t)^C`.

There are three other cases. The minus part is bounded at all odd `ℓ`, with an explicit bound (`ThmBcFree`). The plus
part at the primes of `B(χ)` is bounded given bounds on fixed cyclotomic towers (`PointHV`). At all primes there is a
bound on windows of scales only (`JointWin`, `VWin`).

In the Lean source each of these bounds is a named proposition, and the theorems derive them from other named
propositions. We did not find a declaration that proves any of them without hypotheses.

## Context

By Poitou–Tate duality `Ш¹(E, ℓ, e)` is dual to the kernel of localisation on `H²(𝒪_E[1/ℓ], ℤ_ℓ(e+1))`, which for odd
`ℓ` is the `ℓ`-part of the étale wild kernel of `K_{2e}(𝒪_E)`. The formal statements concern `Ш¹` only and name no
K-group. The question is how large these groups can be, in terms of the conductor and the twist, as the field varies
in a family of growing degree. The source bounds the minus part through Bernoulli numbers and the plus part by other
means.

## Comparison with the literature

We compared the literature with a stronger and differently phrased statement: a bound for all of `Ш¹` outside `B(χ)`,
read as a bound for the étale wild kernels of `K_{2e}(𝒪_E)`, without the condition `q, q′ > r₁, r₂`, with wider window
bounds, with a tower form, and with a claim for all odd `ℓ` when `χ = 1`. The Lean supports none of these extras. It
names no K-groups, it has the two parts as separate propositions, the plus part with the condition on `r₁, r₂`, and no
total, its window bound is narrower, and it has no tower form. What follows bears on the Lean statements through the
two parts. For this family of fields the bound improves on the published ones we found. At odd primes outside a finite
set it is polynomial in the conductor and the twist. That is one exponential below Bayer-Fluckiger–Emery–Houriet 2015
(Theorem 1.1) in the degree and the twist, and further below Soulé 2003 (Theorem 3), with small primes included. Those
published bounds, for K-groups or for étale cohomology, hold for every number field with explicit constants, but at
large primes only. In the discriminant alone the shape of the bound here is theirs, and the minus part is known in
substance.

An independent second check agrees that the bound improves within this family, and corrected us on two points. The two
published ways of setting small primes aside, Soulé's and the quotient by elements of bounded order in
Bayer-Fluckiger–Emery–Houriet, are not equivalent, as we had taken them to be. And summing over the twists is not new
in itself. We are not certain of this comparison, and neither is the second check. We would like to know whether a
bound of this size for the plus part is in print for any family of growing degree, and whether the minus-part bound is
stated character by character for CM fields.

  leaves out the small primes. It holds for every field, explicitly, so neither statement implies the other.

## Sketch of the proof

*Form of the source.* Each bound is a named proposition. Theorems prove implications between them, with the hypotheses
supplied along a chain ending at `vwin_of_row`. `sumOut` proves `SumOut` from the case bounds `CountI`, `CountZ`,
`CountII` and `CountIII`.

*From the field to characters.* `E` is the fixed field of an explicit subgroup `H` of `(ℤ/N)^×` (`hostLevel`). For odd
`ℓ ∉ B(χ)` one has `ℓ ≥ 5` and `ℓ ∤ [E:ℚ]` (`bchiFacts`). Over a discrete valuation ring `O`, finite over `ℤ_ℓ`, with
enough roots of unity, `redPrime` (through `partModel`) gives `length Ш⁺(E, ℓ, e) ≤ Σ_ψ V_ℓ(ψ, e)`, the sum over the
characters `ψ` modulo `N` trivial on `H` with `ψ(−1) = (−1)^e`. Here `V_ℓ(ψ, e)` is the `O`-length, divided by the
ramification index, of the classes in `H¹(G_ℚ, (Frac O/O)(ψ⁻¹χ_ℓ^{−e}))` that are unramified everywhere and zero on
the decomposition groups at the primes dividing `ℓN`.

*Case split* (`cases`). For `ℓ ∉ B(χ)`, Case I is `(ℓ−1) ∤ 2e`. Otherwise `e ≥ 2`, `ℓ ≤ 2t` and `ℓ ∤ N`, and the case
is Z if `ψ = 1`, II if the order of `ψ` is even, and III if the order is odd and `ψ ≠ 1`. The size conditions on
`q, q′` are used here.

*Case I* (`CountI`). Fix `K = ℚ(√−p)`. For one `ψ` the source takes a deformation problem for three-dimensional
conjugate self-dual representations of `G_K` with residual constituents `Ψ̄, 1, Ψ̄⁻¹`, where `Ψ = χ_ℓ^e·ψ`. Its
universal ring, graded by a torus, enters two statements of commutative algebra. `LemmaM′` says that the Selmer group
is no longer than the cotangent module of weight `−1` at the base point. `LemmaM` says that this module has length at
most twice the sum, over the orbits of "two-signed" points `ρ`, of the valuation of `tr ρ(σ) − (Ψ(σ) + 1 + Ψ(σ)⁻¹)`,
for a chosen `σ ∈ G_K`. These points are shown to come from conjugate self-dual automorphic representations of `GL₃`
over `K` of level `N`, or from newforms of weight `2e+1` and level `N·|d_K|` (`OneCharU_s`). This uses rigidity and
automorphy statements and a vanishing statement for Selmer groups of twisted modular forms (`KatoTwoW`), proved
elsewhere in the source. Take `σ` to be a Frobenius above a prime `r`. A count of these representations, polynomial in
`N(e+1)`, and bounds on Hecke eigenvalues give an integer `NN ≠ 0` with `log|NN| ≤ c·#𝒪·(N(e+1))^A·log r` and
`Σ_{ψ∈𝒪} V_ℓ(ψ, e) ≤ 2·v_ℓ(NN)` for each Galois orbit `𝒪` of characters and each admissible `ℓ ≠ r` (`PropGK`). Two
primes `r₁ ≠ r₂` cover all `ℓ`, and `Σ_ℓ log ℓ·v_ℓ(NN) ≤ log|NN|` (`corGK`). These two primes are fixed for `χ` and
must not divide `N`, hence the condition `q, q′ > r₁, r₂`.

*Cases Z, II and III.* `propZ`, a theorem without hypotheses, says that `Ш¹(ℚ, ℓ, e) = 0` for `ℓ ≥ 5`, `e` even and
`(ℓ−1) | 2e`. It uses `h(ℚ(√ℓ)) < ℓ` when `(ℓ−1) ∤ e`. In Cases II and III, `sp4Main_alt` gives
`V_ℓ(ψ, e)·log ℓ ≤ (ℓ·N·e)^C` by the same mechanism, for a symplectic problem of rank four with an auxiliary character
of 2-power conductor. In the assembly (`sumOut`), for each `e < t` the Case I primes are summed at once, and the
others are at most `2t` primes with at most `N` characters each.

*Minus part* (`thmBcFree_of`). For odd `ℓ ∤ [E:ℚ]`, `PsiBound` bounds the `ψ`-component of `Ш⁻` by the valuation of a
generalized Bernoulli number `B_{e+1,ψ}` times an explicit denominator. It is derived from Poitou–Tate duality and a
local orthogonality statement (`UpperTheory.V.ML.iv`). For the odd `ℓ | [E:ℚ]`, `StickBound` says that a power of `ℓ`
of bounded exponent kills `Ш⁻`, whose `ℓ`-torsion has bounded length. The result holds for any abelian `M` in place of
`ℚ(χ)`. With `d = [E:ℚ]` and `C` absolute, it reads
`Σ_{ℓ odd} log ℓ · Σ_{1≤e<t} length Ш⁻(E, ℓ, e) ≤ 3d·t²·log(tN) + C·Σ_ℓ ℓ·d²·t²·log²(tℓN)`, the last sum over the odd
primes dividing `[M:ℚ]`. For odd `ℓ`, `length Ш¹ = length Ш⁺ + length Ш⁻` (`shaLen_eq_add`), so the two parts add.

*Bad primes, the prime 2, and windows.* For `ℓ ∈ B(χ)` the reduction to characters has a loss polynomial in `N·e`
(`redAll`). The components are in Case II, in Case V (`e = 1`, treated by a unitary problem relative to `ℚ(i)` or by
the bound for `H²` below), or in Case IV, where `ψ` is bounded by `Ш¹` of a layer of the cyclotomic `ℓ`-tower of
`ℚ(μ_{ℓf})`. This is why `PointHV` carries a hypothesis `B` on those layers. For such a tower over an abelian field
containing `μ_ℓ` (`μ_4` if `ℓ = 2`), `ABound` says that among the twists `e ≤ Z` at most `R` are exceptional, and that
for the others `Ш¹` of the `m`-th layer has length `O(m + log Z)`. Here `R` comes from the Ferrero–Washington theorem,
which is proved in the source (`FW.ferreroWashington`). At exceptional twists `Ш¹` is bounded by `H²(G_S, ℤ_ℓ(e+1))`
for a fixed layer `L`, with continuous cochains and `G_S` as in the Statement but with `L` in place of `E`. The
identification of this group with the étale cohomology of `𝒪_L[1/ℓ]` is not in the source. For totally complex `L`,
`LemmaK` gives `log log # ≤ (2e[L:ℚ])^C·log(2|d_L|)`. A pigeonhole on scales (`window`) gives `Y ∈ [X^{1/J}, X^{1/2}]`
such that for `x ∈ [Y, Y²]` no exceptional twist lies between `x^η` and `x^κ`. Here `κ ≥ 1` is the exponent bounding
the twists, `t ≤ x^κ`, and `0 < η ≤ κ` is a parameter. The exceptional terms are then at most `exp(x^{4ε/5})`
(`thmBab_of`). `JointWin`, with `0 < ε < 1/52` and `κ = 401`, says the following. For large `X`, for `q, q′` above
`2X^κ+1` and a constant, and with `log(qq′) ≤ X^{ε/(4J)}`, on such a window the 2-part is at most `exp(x^{4ε/5})` and
the plus part at odd primes is at most `(N·t)^C + C·exp(x^{4ε/5})`, for `2^n ≤ x^α` (`α` any fixed real) and
`t ≤ x^κ`. Then `vwinAt_of_joint` adds the minus part. `VWin` bounds the total over all `ℓ` and `e < ⌊x^{401}⌋` by
`√exp(x^ε)`, for `x^{2+8ε} ≤ 2^n ≤ 2x^{2+8ε}` and pairs `q, q′` restricted in size and by congruences. The source has
no single declaration for all of `Ш¹` outside `B(χ)`, and none for fields without the primes `q, q′`.

## Key steps

  `length Ш⁺(E, ℓ, e)` is at most the sum of the `V_ℓ(ψ, e)` over the characters of `Gal(E/ℚ)` of parity `(−1)^e`. All

## Caveats

`SumOut`, `PointHV`, `ThmBcFree`, `JointWin` and `VWin` are definitions of propositions, and we found no theorem that
states any of them without hypotheses. `sumOut`, `point`, `thmBcFree_of`, `jointWin_of`, `vwinAt_of_joint` and
`vwin_of_row` are theorems with proofs, whose hypotheses are other named propositions. We followed the chain from
`vwin_of_row` down to `sumOut` by reading, and each hypothesis is supplied by a theorem of the source, that of
`vwin_of_row` itself in `src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/MainConclusion.lean`. We read the proofs
of `sumOut`, `point`, `thmHV`, `jointWin_of`, `vwinAt_of_joint`, `shaLen_eq_add`, `vvle_of_parts`, `redPrime`,
`cases`, `countZ`, `corGK` and `window`. The rest, namely the deformation rings, automorphy, the counts, `LemmaM`,
`PsiBound`, `StickBound`, `LemmaK` and `ABound`, we read as statements only. A text search of all Lean files found
`sorry` only in `src/Challenge.lean`, which no file imports, and no `axiom`. We compiled nothing.
